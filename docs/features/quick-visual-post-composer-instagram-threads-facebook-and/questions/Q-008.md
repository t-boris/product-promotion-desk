## Question

Should the core, not only the screens, refuse a quick post on a channel that is not Instagram, Threads or Facebook (and a group that mixes the two kinds), and should the card headline be read by the owner-mode safety check like the caption?

## Why it matters

DEC-038, DEC-041 and DEC-046 store the post type explicitly and say the kinds are never mixed, but the rule is enforced only in the channel picker and 'Also post to…'. publications.create, postGroups.create/extend accept a quick post for any channel (Approve then refuses a Reddit post without a title). A card headline typed separately from the caption is not read by draft-check, so a claim in it (a price, an offer, the product name without disclosure) bypasses the warnings the caption gets.

## Options

- **A**: Yes to both: the core refuses a quick post on a non-picture channel and a mixed group (an old article on a Facebook channel stays allowed), and draft-check reads the card headline.
- **B**: Only the core guard; the headline stays unchecked because it is also on the card the owner sees and approves.
- **C**: Neither: the screens are the only door the owner uses; keep as it is.

## Recommended

A
