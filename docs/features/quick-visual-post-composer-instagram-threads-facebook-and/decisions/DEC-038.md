## Context

The form already allows multi-channel posting.

## Alternatives

1. Explicit Article / Quick post switch

## Decision

There is no explicit type switch. The post type follows the chosen channel: Instagram, Threads and Facebook show the quick-post composer; article channels show the existing form.

## Reason

Owner prefers fewer clicks and a composer that appears when the platform is chosen.

## Consequences

The rule for mixed-type channel selections must be defined (open question).
