## Context

The picture must grab attention and be quick to get.

## Alternatives

1. Template card only
2. AI illustration only

## Decision

The default picture of a quick post is a template card drawn by the app (large headline on a coloured background or gradient, chosen by clicks). The owner can instead make an AI illustration (existing MakePicture) or upload their own file.

## Reason

Owner chose both paths, with the template as the fast default.

## Consequences

A card renderer is needed; MakePicture and the picture library are reused for the other two paths.
