## Context

The post type follows the chosen channel (DEC-038), but the form allows several channels at once.

## Alternatives

1. Allow mixing; the caption goes as short text with the picture to article channels
2. Allow mixing; the composer switches to article if any article channel is selected

## Decision

Quick-post channels (Instagram, Threads, Facebook) and article channels (e.g. Reddit) cannot be selected together in one post. Choosing a channel of one kind disables channels of the other kind.

## Reason

Keeps the automatic type by channel predictable, loses no content and leaves the publishing pipeline unchanged.

## Consequences

One topic for both kinds of channel needs two posts. The channel picker must show why a channel is disabled.
