## Context

The owner wants the AI to do more than write text: it should find what topics, ratings and trends interest people now, and act as an ideal marketer who judges every caption and picture against the product's overall goal.

## Alternatives

1. AI only writes caption and picture prompt from the description, with no trend or goal awareness
2. A single separate 'trends' tool outside the caption and picture actions

## Decision

The AI actions for caption and picture in the quick-post composer work in two roles: trend analyst (suggests topics and angles people currently care about) and marketer (checks wording and picture against the product's overall goal). The product goal is an input to every such action. The source of trend data and where the goal is stored are still open.

## Reason

The owner stated that the message, wording and picture matter most and must serve a clear product goal rather than be arbitrary.
