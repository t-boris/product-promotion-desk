## Context

The independent spec review of 0.24.0 listed what REQ-001..003, REQ-006 and DEC-038..048 leave open and the code decided. They are recorded here so the owner can accept or change each. None changes an accepted decision.

## Alternatives

1. Ask the owner for each value before building (rejected: the goal says to ask only what the spec leaves open in a way that changes what is built, and every value here can be changed later by editing one table)

## Decision

Card: three styles (bold, quote, minimal), six colours (indigo, sunset, ocean, forest, berry, graphite), three shapes drawn 1080 px wide (square 1080, portrait 1350 = 4:5, wide 566 = 1.91:1, the two ends of what Instagram takes); the headline is the first non-empty line of the caption, at most 140 characters (the field stops there, an emoji counts as one); a headline too long to fit is shrunk and then cut with an ellipsis; breaks and cuts never split an emoji sequence. The card file is named card-<first five words>.png with library origin 'card'; an older card stays in the library when a new one is drawn. Composer: nothing ticked shows the article form; the screen's own channel is pre-ticked; an empty caption is allowed with a picture; a quick post is plain text (Markdown is not offered); the caption counter uses the tightest limit of the ticked platforms; an own picture can be replaced but not removed (the card is the way back); the form of each kind keeps its own state when the kind is switched. A saved card post whose caption changed without the owner (the group's Adapt with AI) is offered for saving and is drawn again, so the approved picture is the shown one. Names: a quick post is named by the first 8 words of its caption (at most 80 characters), fallbacks 'Picture post' in lists and 'New publication' in the review card. Picture rules (src/shared/post-pictures.ts): Instagram PNG or JPEG, 8 MB, 4:5 to 1.91:1; Threads PNG or JPEG, 8 MB, up to 10:1; Facebook PNG, JPEG or GIF; a 0.5% slack on ratios; WebP is refused everywhere although the library accepts it; Instagram's 30-hashtag limit is not checked; scheduled and automatic sends of a quick post are refused. AI-suggested and content-mix drafts for Instagram, Threads and Facebook channels stay articles (DEC-038 is about the composer).

## Reason

The spec fixes what the owner can do and what is stored but not these values; each is a one-line change (a table or a constant) and none of them is stored in a way that cannot be changed later. The limits and picture rules come from public guides and are unproven on the real forms.

## Consequences

Accepting this makes the values part of the spec; changing one is an edit of src/shared/quick-post.ts or src/shared/post-pictures.ts. Real-site acceptance of the picture rules stays open (issue #8).
