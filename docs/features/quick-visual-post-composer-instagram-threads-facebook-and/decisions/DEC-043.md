## Context

The marketer needs an overall goal to evaluate wording and picture.

## Alternatives

1. New 'Promotion goal' field on the product card
2. Goal from knowledge, refined per post in the composer

## Decision

The marketer role judges the caption and picture against the overall promotion goal derived from the product's approved knowledge. No new 'promotion goal' field is added to the product card and no per-post goal field is added.

## Reason

The owner chose no new fields; the existing approved knowledge is the source.

## Consequences

The goal may be implicit in the knowledge, so assessment quality depends on how well knowledge is written; the product card stays unchanged.
