## Finding

The card is drawn by the app, but the spec does not say whether it is rendered to an image file, saved into the picture library, or when. It also does not say where the headline comes from, since there is no title field, or whether it stays in sync with the caption. Sending requires an actual image.

> A new quick post starts with a template card made from the caption or headline

## Possible interpretations

- The card is rendered to a PNG and stored in the library on save
- The card is kept as parameters and rendered at send time

## Resolution

При сохранении черновика карточка рендерится в PNG и кладётся в библиотеку картинок продукта как обычная картинка поста. Заголовок берётся из первой строки подписи, пока владелец не отредактирует его отдельно в композере. — see DEC-045.
