## Statement

The composer helps the owner think through the message and wording. Caption and picture each have their own separate AI action, using approved product knowledge. The AI acts in two roles: a trend analyst and a goal-aware marketer. Trend analyst: on the owner's request it searches current trends and topics people care about through the AI browser (DEC-035) and suggests an angle for the post. Marketer: it checks the caption and picture against the product's overall promotion goal, which is taken from the approved product knowledge (no new product field). If the search is unavailable or fails, the owner is told so and can continue without trends.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The caption action and the picture action are separate buttons; there is no single one-shot action.
- [ ] A trend search runs only when the owner asks for it and uses the AI browser; the owner sees that a search is in progress and its result (topics/angle) before it changes the post.
- [ ] The marketer's assessment and suggestions refer to the product goal derived from approved product knowledge; no new goal field is added to the product card.
- [ ] If the AI browser search fails or is unavailable, the owner sees a clear message and can still write the caption and make the picture without trends.
- [ ] The AI never overwrites the caption or picture without the owner's action, and an Undo is available as in existing AI fields.
