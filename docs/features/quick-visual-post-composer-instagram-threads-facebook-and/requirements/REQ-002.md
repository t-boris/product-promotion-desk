## Statement

The quick-post composer builds a post from exactly one picture and a short caption, shows it live as the chosen platform (Instagram, Threads, Facebook) would show it, and saves it as a draft through the existing draft and approval flow. A quick post is stored as a draft with an explicit post-type field (values: article and quick post; DEC-046). For a quick post the caption is stored in the text field, the title is empty and not required, and the template card parameters (style, colour, shape, headline) are kept in a structured field of the draft. Existing drafts get the default type article through a migration. Review, approval and preview screens choose their layout by post type, not by guessing from an empty title; where a label is needed for a quick post, the first words of the caption are shown. When the picture is a template card, saving the draft renders the card to a PNG and adds it to the product's picture library as an ordinary post picture (DEC-045), and that PNG is the post's single picture. The card headline is taken from the first line of the caption until the owner edits the headline separately in the composer. Because the saved card is an ordinary post picture, approval and sending need no pipeline changes for it. Editing a card after saving requires a new render. How a saved card is re-edited is specified in REQ-003; the draft storage format is settled by DEC-046. The picture in a quick post must be deliverable to each of the three platforms: sending pictures to Instagram, Threads and Facebook is in scope (see the separate picture-sending requirement, which has acceptance criteria per platform and is linked to this one), and only the picture-sending part of the publishing pipeline is changed. The approval flow and the rest of the pipeline stay unchanged, and DEC-030 and DEC-035 remain valid and are extended, not replaced. The data migration and the storage layer changes required by DEC-046 are in scope.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The composer has one picture slot and one caption field, and no mandatory title
- [ ] A live preview shows the post in the layout of the selected platform (Instagram, Threads or Facebook)
- [ ] Saving creates a normal draft per ticked channel; nothing is published before approval
- [ ] The caption length limit of the platform is shown while typing
- [ ] The draft has an explicit post-type field with the values article and quick post
- [ ] A draft created in the quick-post composer is saved with post type quick post, and a draft created in the article form is saved with post type article
- [ ] For a quick post the caption is stored in the text field and the title is stored empty; saving with an empty title is not rejected
- [ ] The template card parameters (style, colour, shape, headline) of a quick post are stored in a structured field of the draft
- [ ] A migration sets the post type article on all existing drafts, and after the migration existing drafts open and behave as before
- [ ] Review, approval and preview screens choose their layout by the post-type field and not by checking whether the title is empty
- [ ] A quick post with an empty title is shown correctly on review, approval and preview screens, and where a label is needed the first words of the caption are shown instead of the title
- [ ] The storage layer and every place that reads drafts handle the post-type field and the structured card-parameters field
- [ ] Saving a quick-post draft whose picture is a template card renders the card to a PNG
- [ ] The rendered card PNG is added to the product's picture library as an ordinary post picture and is attached to the draft as its single picture
- [ ] The card headline is taken from the first line of the caption until the owner edits the headline separately in the composer
- [ ] Once the owner has edited the headline separately, later changes to the first line of the caption do not change the headline
- [ ] Changing the card (headline, style or colour) after the draft was saved takes effect only after a new render of the PNG
- [ ] Approval and sending of a saved template-card post work with no changes to the approval flow or the pipeline beyond picture sending, because the card is an ordinary post picture
- [ ] A quick post created in the composer can be sent with its picture to Instagram, Threads and Facebook once approved, as specified by the linked picture-sending requirement and its per-platform acceptance criteria
- [ ] Where picture sending to a platform already works, it is not reworked; only the gaps found per platform are filled
- [ ] Changes to the publishing pipeline are limited to picture sending; the approval flow and all other pipeline behaviour remain unchanged
- [ ] REQ-002 links to the picture-sending requirement
- [ ] REQ-002 links to REQ-003, which specifies re-editing of a saved card
