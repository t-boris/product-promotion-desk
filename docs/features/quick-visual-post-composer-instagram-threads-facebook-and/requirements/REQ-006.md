## Statement

The system shall publish an approved quick post (one picture plus a caption, REQ-002) with its picture on Instagram, Threads and Facebook. For each platform the first step is to find out, from the code and by test, whether the existing sending path (connector or the AI browser, DEC-035 of the product spec) already does it, and to record the result; only the gaps found are filled (DEC-044). Only picture sending changes: the approval flow and the rest of the publishing pipeline stay as they are, and DEC-030 and DEC-035 of the product spec are extended, not replaced. What is sent is exactly the approved text and the approved picture file; nothing is published before approval, and a platform that cannot take the picture refuses the draft at Approve with words, before the press, not after the dialog was opened.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] For each of Instagram, Threads and Facebook the result of the check 'does an approved post with one picture and a caption already go out correctly' is recorded in this requirement or in a decision, with the code path that carries it
- [ ] Instagram: an approved quick post is sent with its picture and caption; the picture is attached in the platform's post form and the caption is typed as approved
- [ ] Threads: an approved quick post is sent with its picture and caption; the picture is attached in the platform's post form and the caption is typed as approved
- [ ] Facebook: an approved quick post is sent with its picture and caption; the picture is attached in the platform's post form and the caption is typed as approved
- [ ] Where picture sending to a platform already works, it is not reworked; only the gaps found per platform are filled
- [ ] A draft whose picture the platform cannot take (missing picture on Instagram, a picture of an unsupported type or size) is refused at Approve with a message that names the reason
- [ ] The text and the picture that are sent are the ones the approval hash binds, and a changed picture or caption sends nothing until approved again
- [ ] Changes to the publishing pipeline are limited to picture sending; the approval flow and all other pipeline behaviour remain unchanged
- [ ] Automated tests cover the picture-sending path for each of the three platforms
