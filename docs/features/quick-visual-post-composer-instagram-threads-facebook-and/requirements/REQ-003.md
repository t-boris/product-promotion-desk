## Statement

The quick-post picture is by default a template card drawn by the app (large headline on a coloured background or gradient; style, colour and shape chosen by clicks). The card headline is taken from the first line of the caption until the owner edits the headline separately in the composer. A quick post is stored as a draft with an explicit post-type field (value quick post): the caption is stored in the text field, the title is empty and not required, and the template card parameters (style, colour, shape, headline) are kept in a structured field of the draft. When the quick-post draft is saved, the template card is rendered to a PNG and added to the product's picture library as an ordinary post picture, and the draft references that PNG as its picture, so approval and sending need no pipeline changes. Style, colour, shape and headline edits are available before saving and also on an already saved card, using the stored structured card parameters; editing a saved card creates a new rendered PNG on the next save. The owner can instead make an AI illustration through the existing picture maker or upload their own file.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] A new quick post starts with a template card made from the caption or headline, with no AI engine needed.
- [x] By default the card headline is the first line of the caption, and it updates as that first line changes.
- [x] The owner can edit the card headline separately in the composer; after that the headline no longer follows the first line of the caption.
- [x] Style, colour and shape of the card can be changed with clicks and the preview updates at once.
- [x] Buttons for an AI illustration and for the owner's own file replace the card as the post's one picture.
- [x] A quick-post draft is saved with the post-type field set to quick post, the caption in the text field, and an empty title that is not required for saving.
- [x] The template card parameters (style, colour, shape, headline) are saved in the structured card-parameters field of the draft.
- [x] Saving a quick-post draft with a template card renders the card to a PNG and adds it to the product's picture library as an ordinary post picture.
- [x] The saved draft references the rendered PNG as its picture, so approval and sending work without changes to the pipeline.
- [x] When the picture is an AI illustration or an uploaded file, no card PNG is rendered on save.
- [x] Opening a saved quick post with a template card in the composer restores its style, colour, shape and headline from the structured card-parameters field.
- [x] Editing the style, colour, shape or headline of an already saved card and saving again renders a new PNG and adds it to the picture library, and the draft references the new PNG.
- [x] A complete quick post can be made in a few steps.
