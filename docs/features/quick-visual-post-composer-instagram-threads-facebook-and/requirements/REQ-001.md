## Statement

The post type follows the channel chosen in the New publication form: Instagram, Threads and Facebook show the dedicated quick-post composer (one picture plus a short caption); article channels show the existing article form. Channels of the two kinds cannot be selected together: selecting one kind disables the other kind in the channel picker. The chosen post type is saved with the draft in an explicit post-type field with the values article and quick post (not inferred from an empty title). In the quick-post composer the title is not required and is stored empty, and the caption is stored in the text field.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Selecting Instagram, Threads or Facebook shows the quick-post composer
- [ ] Selecting an article channel shows the existing article form
- [ ] With a quick-post channel selected, article channels are disabled, and the other way round, with a visible reason
- [ ] Several channels of the same kind can be selected together
- [ ] Clearing all channels re-enables every channel
- [ ] A draft saved from the quick-post composer has the post-type field set to quick post
- [ ] A draft saved from the article form has the post-type field set to article
- [ ] The quick-post composer does not require a title: a draft can be saved with an empty title, and the title is stored empty
- [ ] In a quick post the caption is stored in the draft's text field
