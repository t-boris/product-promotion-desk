## Context

Three items of docs/design/ideal-promotion-app.md depend on questions the document asks the owner (3: how many channels at once; 5: is the product software; 6: may a week with no time carry a re-cut). The goal says to implement without pausing, so each is built as a choice the owner makes on the screen and that is off until then.

## Alternatives

1. Pick the numbers for the owner (two channels, re-cut always on): decides what the document asks them
2. State each venue's rules and waiting time in the application: rules change and the owner refused a rules reader (DEC-050)

## Decision

1. Limit on channels in work: on a channel's Results tab (trial card) the line "Channels of this product: 6 in work · your limit is 2 (4 over)" with "Set my limit". A number shown; it blocks and pauses nothing. 2. "A week with no time" card under the Posts calendar with one switch: while on, the content mix of the product rests and each channel that has sent a post gets one draft in 7 days that says an earlier post again in a new form with no new fact; drafts only, replies as usual. 3. "Launch venues" card under the Posts calendar: a tick "This product is software" opens five venues, each with the venue's own page and the owner's own checklist (rules read, page ready, day chosen, launched). The application states and reads no venue's rules and posts nothing there.

## Reason

An off-by-default choice answers the question on the owner's own press and decides nothing for them; a re-cut is a draft, so no post goes out by itself.

## Consequences

The limit is not used by the AI's channel ideas yet. The re-cut uses the channel's earlier posts as the AI already receives them.
