## Context

DEC-010 section 3 required a text that names the product (or its website) to say the author is connected with it, decided in code at every approval and send, for the owner's text too, plus a model flag and an automatic-reply condition.

## Alternatives

1. Keep it for AI-written text only and make it a one-line heads-up for the owner's text (the previous proposal)
2. Keep the check but make it advice with a one-press way through

## Decision

Nothing in the application checks, asks for or blocks on a statement of the author's connection to the product. Removed entirely: the check at approval and at send (owner's text and AI text), the gate block, the "Check before you approve" finding (both "unsaid" and "denies"), the reminders on AI drafts and suggested posts, the model's disclosesConnection flag and the automatic-reply condition built on it, the card hint. The AI prompts say only: do not claim to be an independent user and do not deny a connection with the product; say nothing about a connection unless the owner's own text does. Unchanged: the single-login rule (one login per product speaks in a discussion), the role/profile wording, the community-rule restriction, and the safety checks (prohibited statements, prices, links, credentials).

## Reason

The owner decided on 2026-11-21 ("Remove this entirely from the logic"): they post under their own accounts on their own initiative and are not asked to prove or state who they work for. The rule, written to stop the AI posing as an independent user, ended up blocking the owner's own comment ("Must be fixed") over a product link they did not even write.

## Consequences

A reply or post that names the product is no longer held for the owner for that reason: in automatic mode such a reply can now be sent without the owner reading it (the other automatic-reply guards still apply). Communities that expect an affiliation to be said are now the owner's own risk. Old items keep their stored reason text as plain text; no migration.
