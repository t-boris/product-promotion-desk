## Decision

Show active scheduled comments/replies (including scheduled Feed answers with scheduledAt set and state neither confirmed nor cancelled) in the Posts list and Calendar alongside standalone posts and post groups. Scheduled comments carry kind: "comment", a "Comment" badge in list rows, calendar cells, and day agenda entries, display reply context ("Reply to …" / "Comment on …"), are searchable by reply text or target context, and open their full review and schedule controls in PostDetail without showing "Also post to…".

## Reason

Owner approved showing scheduled comments in Posts (list and calendar) so all outgoing scheduled activity (posts and comments alike) is visible and manageable from one unified schedule and calendar view without cluttering Posts with unscheduled replies.
