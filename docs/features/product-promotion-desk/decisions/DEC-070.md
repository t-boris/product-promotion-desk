## Context

Item 5 of docs/design/ideal-promotion-app.md: themes and campaigns. One piece in versions per channel exists as post groups (DEC-032) and its recap is on Review (0.68.0); nothing named a subject over several pieces.

## Alternatives

1. Attach each post to a campaign by hand: a field on every post and on the approval; the days already say which posts belong
2. Let the AI plan the whole campaign's posts at once: automatic standalone posts, which the owner refused

## Decision

A card "Campaigns" under the Posts calendar, per product: a name, what it says (a sentence or two), a first and a last day (at most 92 days). While a campaign runs, the AI is given its name and words when it proposes a post, as the owner's topic and never as a fact about the product; when two overlap, the one that started first. Each campaign that runs or is over shows what the application sent in its days and what the sites show under it. A campaign schedules, approves and posts nothing. In the code it is "theme" (shared/post-themes.ts); the screen says "campaign" because "Theme" already names the appearance in Settings.

## Reason

The smallest thing that makes several posts one subject: a period and a sentence the AI follows, with an honest count.

## Consequences

A post sent in the days of two overlapping campaigns counts in both. Campaign days are not drawn on the calendar grid yet.
