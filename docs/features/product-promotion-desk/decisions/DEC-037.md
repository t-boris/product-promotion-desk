## Context

Owner's feedback 2026-11-15: Attention is a long unclear list, Products is a long column with no graphics, it is unclear which product a thing belongs to, Opportunities does not show what happens in the channels, Publications cannot be filtered or sorted, and Open browser must be closed before the AI can work. A proposal was written in docs/design/ui-redesign-plan.md with five open decisions D1-D5 and defaults. The owner then issued the goal "Implement what docs/design/ui-redesign-plan.md specifies" (2026-11-15), which accepts the plan with its defaults.

## Alternatives

1. Keep the object-type navigation and only polish each screen (rejected by the owner: the structure is the problem).
2. Introduce a Brand entity above Product (not chosen: the plan's default is the product switcher only; a brand would be a new data model).
3. Keep the one-controller lock on the account browser (rejected as the default of phase D).

## Decision

The plan in docs/design/ui-redesign-plan.md is the specification. Answers recorded: D1 the sidebar structure of section 4 (product switcher, Overview, Inbox, Feed, Posts, Channels, Accounts, Setup, Log, Settings); D2 no Brand entity, only the product switcher; D3 the one-controller lock on the account browser is removed (phase D; it supersedes the parts of DEC-026 and DEC-028 that make the AI wait while the owner browses); D4 the Feed reads Reddit first (new posts, replies to me, mentions), reading Instagram, Facebook and Threads stays a separate decision; D5 the order A, B, C, D, E. The visual-redesign scope of DEC-023 (no changes to texts, routes, API, core) does not apply to this change; V-03 (the sidebar's seven items) and the frozen QA texts of V-21 are rewritten with the screens that change. Every other rule of DEC-023 holds: tokens only, 28 px controls, one filled button per screen, light and dark, no new dependencies. The safety rules do not change: approval bound to exact content before any send, the send gate, one voice per discussion (DEC-010), drafts-only content mix, unknown results reconciled by the owner.

## Reason

The owner asked for the plan to be implemented. It records its defaults openly, and each phase is released and proved separately, so a wrong default is cheap to change.

## Consequences

Routes and section names change; old hashes redirect. QA scenarios and the screen baselines are updated with each phase. Phase D needs probes in real Chrome and Electron before release. Phase E needs a read-only probe of what a signed-in Reddit session offers before any build. Work is done on a branch with a pull request, not on main (this supersedes the bootstrapping rule of straight-to-main).</consequences>
