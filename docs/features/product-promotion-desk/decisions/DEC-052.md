## Context

With Natal Mystic chosen, the Accounts screen listed Sirius Art Official and its five logins next to Boris Velnarov. The owner asked why another product's accounts were shown and whether accounts of two products were being mixed (2026-11-23). DEC-015 had made accounts one library shared by all products and the screen listed every login. Since then every person (profile) is made for one product (DEC-019), and the Accounts item sits in the product part of the sidebar, under the product switcher.

## Alternatives

1. Keep one library and fold the other products' people under 'Other products' below the open product's
2. Keep one library, move 'Accounts' in the sidebar next to Log and Settings and label each person with its product

## Decision

The Accounts screen and the count beside 'Accounts' in the top bar show only the open product's people and logins. A person belongs to a product when it was made for it or one of its logins serves a channel of the product, and it is listed with all of its logins. A person made for no product counts for the product its logins were made for. A login without a person belongs to the product it was made for or serves. A person or login tied to no product and serving no channel is listed in every product. A new profile starts on the open product and a new login is recorded as made for it. A link to a login of another product (an Inbox item) opens that product. DEC-015 keeps the rest: an account can still serve a channel of another product, chosen where the channel is connected with the existing warning, and it is then listed in both products. The rule lives in src/shared/account-scope.ts (peopleOfProduct, productOfAccount).

## Reason

The owner chose this layout ('Только люди продукта') over the two alternatives on 2026-11-23. Everything else under the product switcher is the open product's; a screen there that lists every product's people reads as mixing products.

## Consequences

Released as 0.50.0. The Accounts screen loads channels.list (all products) to apply the rule; the top bar loads profiles.list and channels.list. accounts.list and profiles.list still return everything, because the channel picker needs every account of a platform. DEC-015's sentence "accounts.list always lists every account ... a top-level Accounts screen" now holds for the API only.
