## Context

Item 9 of docs/design/ideal-promotion-app.md (the owner's goal of 2026-11-27): people ask AI assistants what to use; the owner had no way to see whether the product is named there and which sites the answers rest on.

## Alternatives

1. Ask with the product in the prompt: the answer would be led and worthless
2. Let the AI say whether the product was named: the application can check that itself, exactly
3. Query ChatGPT or Google's AI answers directly: no access the owner has; out of reach without their keys

## Decision

On the Review screen a card keeps up to twenty questions a buyer would ask (the AI may propose them from the approved passport; nothing is kept until the owner saves). "Ask now" puts each question to the application's AI engine with web search, one request a question, with no word about the product, and keeps the answer, the names it recommends and the pages it used. The application, not the AI, works out whether the product's name is among them and whether its site is a source, and counts the names and sites named most. Checks are kept and compared with the one before; the scheduler asks again when the last check is thirty days old (never the first time). The card says plainly that this is a sign, not a measurement: a person asking ChatGPT or Google may see another answer. What the engine read is strangers' text: stored and shown as plain text, never followed and never used in a post.

## Reason

The research: AI answers are a discovery surface; the useful output is a to-do list of where to be present.

## Consequences

One engine (the one chosen in Settings) is asked, not every installed one. A monthly check costs as many searching AI requests as there are questions. No Inbox item is raised by a check.
