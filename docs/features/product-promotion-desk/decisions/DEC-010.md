## Context

The owner added (2026-11-08, translated from Russian): for each product there is a channel and credentials to connect with, but for the same product on a channel such as Reddit the owner may connect with different credentials, writing as an astrologer in one case, as a developer in another, and so on; there can be several per product, and the credentials should be thought up. Records checked: SRC-001 section 1 (an account is the user's own account on a platform), DEC-009 (own login per product, reuse only by consent), REQ-003 (the account's own comments do not trigger replies), REQ-004 (approval is bound to the account), the current code (several logins of one product on one platform are already possible, a login has no role, a comment is treated as own only for the login of its channel, and nothing stops two logins of a product answering the same comment). Three follow-up questions were answered by the owner, all with the recommended option.

## Alternatives

1. Several logins without roles, one voice for all (rejected: the owner wants a different voice per login)
2. The one-login-per-discussion guard switchable per channel (rejected: the owner chose the mandatory guard)
3. No automatic disclosure, the owner decides in each text (rejected: the owner chose to disclose by default)

## Decision

1. Role: every login of a product may have a role, a name plus a voice (how it writes and what it may talk about, for example astrologer or developer). Drafts for a channel are written in the voice of the login connected to that channel. The AI proposes roles and username ideas from the approved passport. The owner creates the logins on the platform: the application never registers accounts, never sees or stores passwords, and never creates accounts on its own. 2. Guard, mandatory and not switchable: at most one login of a product speaks in a given discussion; a comment written by any login of the product (known identity) never triggers a reply by another of its logins. It is applied when comments are ingested and drafts are made, and enforced again by the SendGate at send time. 3. Disclosure: a draft that mentions the product states, in one short phrase and in the role's voice, that the author is connected with it; a role never presents itself as an independent user. The owner can edit any text before approving it, as before.

## Reason

It gives the owner the several voices they described while keeping the logins theirs and their sends authorized, and it prevents the pattern that platforms treat as manipulation (own logins answering each other or the same comment) and that leads to bans.

## Consequences

Account gains an optional role (migration in the channels range); new API for setting and suggesting roles; drafting prompts carry the voice and the disclosure rule; ingestion treats comments of every known login of the product as own; the SendGate denies a second login of the product in a discussion that another login already spoke in. The Channels tab shows and edits roles. REQ-010 carries the testable criteria.
