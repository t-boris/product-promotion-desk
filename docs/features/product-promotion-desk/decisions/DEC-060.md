## Context

Item 5 of docs/design/ideal-promotion-app.md (the owner's goal of 2026-11-27): seasonal content has to be ready weeks ahead on search-like surfaces, and nothing in the application knew a date was coming.

## Alternatives

1. Let the AI name the dates: it would guess moments and invent events
2. Planets' retrogrades and the moon's sign: not offered, they need an ephemeris the application does not have
3. Prepare drafts automatically when a lead time opens: not built; the owner presses

## Decision

Per product the owner chooses calendars to follow: moon phases, sign seasons, equinoxes and solstices (all computed in the application, Meeus' main terms, right to about a quarter of an hour), gift dates (a fixed list of rules, the dates used in the United States), and adds dates of their own (every year or once). The Posts calendar names the dates in its cells and lists them eleven weeks ahead in a card "Dates ahead", each with the product's active channels and when each one's time to prepare comes: 35 days before for Pinterest, 14 for YouTube, 7 elsewhere. "Prepare a post" asks the AI for a draft for one date and one channel, telling it only the name and the day; the draft is approved like any other. One Inbox item per product says when dates are due with nothing prepared; "Skip this date" ends the asking for that date. Nothing is prepared or posted without the owner's press.

## Reason

The research: Pinterest asks for seasonal content 4-6 weeks ahead; a niche like astrology has a calendar that writes the content plan by itself.

## Consequences

Lead times are fixed per platform, not set by the owner. Gift dates are one list (United States); no other country's dates. Themes and campaigns (one main piece with native versions per channel, built on post groups) are not built. The draft carries no planned day of its own: the owner schedules it.
