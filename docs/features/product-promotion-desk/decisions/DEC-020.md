## Context

0.5.0 kept the profile inside one account. The owner corrected it: a profile is a person, made once; accounts are its logins on Facebook, Instagram or any platform; another profile may hold a login on the same platform, so a platform can have several profiles and accounts.

## Alternatives

1. Keep the profile as a part of each account (0.5.0): the same person has to be made again on every platform and can drift between them.

## Decision

A profile (person, purpose, strategy, role and voice, names, birth date, gender, place, about, website, picture, platforms) is a separate entity. An account has an optional profile_id; a profile has at most one account per platform (unique on profile and platform). The role and voice live on the profile and an account of a profile speaks with it; a login without a profile keeps its own role. Missions (signup, fill in the profile) take the approved profile of the account. The AI finds the profile editor on any site itself and matches the fields by meaning.

## Reason

It matches how the owner thinks about the work (one person, many platforms) and avoids making the same person again for each login. Confirmed by the owner: one account per platform for a profile, role and voice on the profile.

## Consequences

Migration 207 turns each 0.5.0 account profile into a profile of its own and links the account to it. The role editor of a login that belongs to a profile is read-only. Missions refuse an account without an approved profile.
