## Context

Owner answered Q-008 with Option A and asked to finish remaining items from Section 6, including persisting and displaying when sign-in was last checked and what was seen.

## Alternatives

1. Keep quick-post platform/mixing checks only in the renderer UI and leave card headlines unchecked by drafts.check; keep sign-in check results only in ephemeral logs.

## Decision

Enforce quick-post platform and non-mixing group rules in core, include quick card headlines in drafts.check, and persist last_checked_at and last_check_signal on accounts (migration 213) for display in the Sign-in and API key blocks.

## Reason

Prevents invalid post shapes or mixed groups from bypassing the UI, ensures card headlines receive the same owner-mode safety warnings as captions, and gives the owner direct visibility into the last sign-in check without hunting through logs.

## Consequences

Core rejects invalid quick-post channels and mixed groups at the API boundary (while keeping article drafts on Facebook/Threads/Instagram allowed); drafts.check inspects card headlines in both saved drafts and live forms; accounts table gains last_checked_at and last_check_signal (migration 213) shown in Sign-in and API key blocks.
