## Context

The plan's fact D8 ("is the Instagram account professional; is Facebook a Page or a personal profile") was asked once per product. The owner answered on 2026-11-27: a product has several profiles, each with its own accounts (for example three Meta accounts: one business, two personal), so the question belongs to each account; and every time an account, a bot or a channel is connected the application must give the instructions itself, with links that open the application's own browser at the right place.

## Alternatives

1. Ask the kind once per product (fact D8 as written): wrong for a product with a business and personal accounts
2. Let the AI find out the kind by looking at the account's settings: a read of the owner's settings nobody asked for; the owner knows the answer
3. Instructions in the chat or a document: the owner asked for them in the application, at the moment of connecting

## Decision

An account has a kind (accounts.kind, one of the kinds its platform lists: Instagram "Professional (business or creator)" or "Personal"; Facebook "A Page" or "A personal profile"; null = not said). The form "New account" asks it ("Kind of account", with "Not sure yet"); it can be changed on the account's page. The Sign-in tab of every account starts with the block "How to connect this account": numbered steps chosen by platform, kind and way of working (browser or API), written as data of the platform (its preset, or its API connector for a Telegram bot); a step with an address has a button that opens that page in the account's own browser. A platform that wrote no steps gets the two steps every login has. The buttons only open pages: nothing is filled in or pressed for the owner. Sketch: https://claude.ai/artifact/9ZyNGsdrvbDcwRd9Ag5osC.

## Reason

The owner's order: many accounts of different kinds, instructions every time, in the internal browser. One mechanism (data per platform, one steps component) instead of text per screen.

## Consequences

The kind changes only the steps shown; both kinds are still worked in the browser. The steps for connecting Meta's own API per business account are not written: that connection (phases F-H) is not built. Menu names on Instagram and Facebook are as third-party guides describe them in 2026 and were not seen on the owner's accounts; the address web.telegram.org/k/#@BotFather was not opened live.
