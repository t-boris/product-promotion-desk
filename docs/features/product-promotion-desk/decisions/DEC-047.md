## Context

After 0.27.0 the owner's three Reddit channels (one account) were still looked at one by one, each scheduled look ended red ("The AI ended without saying how it went", up to 6 times in a row, nothing recorded since 02:48), and the AI opened Reddit searches nobody asked for (the brief carried the first three of the channel's saved queries as search pages). The owner: "AI cannot do parallel calls, that is grim, fix it urgently", then "All 3" to the plan, then "Why up to 3? Why a limit at all?".

## Alternatives

1. Keep one look per account and make them faster: does not fix the hour, the red statuses or the confusion.
2. A cap of 3 looks per account in the AI service: repeats the scheduler's limit and the owner asked what it was for.
3. No limit anywhere: ten channels on one account would open ten windows and ten Codex processes at once; the scheduler's 3 stays until a live run shows what Reddit tolerates.

## Decision

1) A look that ends by itself without its report logs how many tools it called, how many posts it recorded, the last page (address and title, never the text) and the AI's last words, and the channel's status quotes the last words. 2) A scheduled look opens the channel's newest page (Reddit: /new/) and the channel's own page, goes back to the last whole look (30 days the first time), and does not search; the channel's queries only tell the AI what the owner follows, and searching stays in Look around. 3) Looks (watch, research) of one account run side by side, each in a window titled with what it looks at; a mission that acts (sign-in, profile, join, publication) takes the whole account and never runs beside a look; Stop on the account stops everything on it; the pause between two page openings is shared by all the looks of the account. There is no cap in the AI service: the scheduler's existing limit of 3 channel checks at a time is the only one.

## Reason

The owner has several channels on one account, so a per-account queue turned a few-minute job into the best part of an hour and made every postponed look look like a failure. A look only reads, nobody is asked anything, and each has a page of its own since 0.27.0, so nothing needs the queue except a mission that acts. A shared pace keeps the account from opening pages faster than one sign-in ever did. A second cap in the AI service would only repeat the scheduler's.

## Consequences

Page operations of one account still go through one queue (each is short); the AI's own thinking runs in parallel. Windows of looks sit on top of each other (no cascade yet), told apart by their titles. The cause of the red looks is not established until a look is read in the new log line. Unproven live: three looks of the owner's Reddit account side by side, and whether Reddit tolerates them.
