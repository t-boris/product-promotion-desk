## Context

The plan's phase F includes a video to a Page; it was left out of 0.78.0 for three gaps in Meta's pages. Read again on 2026-11-28 (Graph API v27.0): the overview says every request goes to graph.facebook.com and calls graph-video.facebook.com deprecated; the reference of a Page's videos documents one request with the Page's own token (the file as `source`, the words as `description`); the answer names the video, not the post, and no page of Meta's documentation says how the one leads to the other (a `post_id` field of a video is only in Meta's SDK, not in its documentation).

## Alternatives

1. Keep a video not built: the last piece of phase F stays out though every request it needs is documented
2. Read `post_id` of the video: present only in Meta's SDK, on no documentation page
3. The resumable upload through the app's number: needs a person's token, the application holds only the Page's
4. Give a video post to Facebook's own schedule too: a risk of a second copy when the kept post cannot be found

## Decision

One video with the post's words is sent in one request to the Page's videos on graph.facebook.com with the Page's token. The post is then looked for where the application already looks for a post whose answer was lost: among the Page's own posts, by its words and its time, every five seconds for three minutes; found, the send is confirmed with the post's address and its numbers are read like any post's. Not found in that time, the send is "not known" and is looked for again by the same mechanism every unknown send has; nothing is ever sent twice by the application. A post holds photos or one video, not both, and the preview says so before the approval. A post with a video and a time is not given to Facebook's own schedule: it goes out from this application at its time (the connector says which posts its platform keeps: `ownSchedule.takes`).

## Reason

Every step the application takes is in Meta's documentation; the step that is not (video to post) is replaced by a documented reading of the Page's posts, which the application already trusts for lost answers. Giving a video to Facebook's schedule would need the kept post's number, and nothing says a kept video shows among the scheduled posts: a video the application could not find there would be sent a second time at its time.

## Consequences

A video that Facebook processes for longer than ten minutes comes back to review as "not found", and the owner looks at the Page before sending again. A post with a video and a time needs this Mac on at that time. Meta's reference states no limit of size or length for the one-request form: a refusal is said in Facebook's words. Unproven with a real Page: that the post of a video carries the video's description as its words, and how long processing takes. Reels are not built.
