## Context

Owner, 2026-11-15, looking at r/astrology: Read posts was Unavailable ("access denied") and the app had not joined the community itself ("Join Community, он не умеет это делать, это странно"). The B1 rule (DEC-024 era) is that the AI does nothing to an account that no approved content asked for: Join, Follow, Vote and the like are refused by the page layer in every post mission, and no screen said so. The owner answered "Делай всё: диагностику, членство и вступление".

## Alternatives

1. Join automatically as part of assessing or posting (rejected: the B1 rule, and a join is hard to undo and visible on the account's profile)
2. Let the AI click anything while joining and trust its report (rejected: page text can steer a model; only the tool layer holds)
3. Require a ticket like a post (rejected: tickets bind content to be published; there is none, and a second approval after the owner's press would be ceremony)
4. Change how the post list is read to avoid the 403 now (rejected: the cause is unknown; the next Assess shows Reddit's real answer first)
5. Join through Reddit's API from the connector (rejected: it needs a write call with the account's session outside the visible browser, and the owner asked for the AI to use the page)

## Decision

1) A refusal explains itself: the capability text says what Reddit answered in safe words (JSON reason/message as plain words, a fixed phrase for a known block page, never raw page text), one refused post list is not repeated as three problems (Read comments and Detect new activity say they wait for it). 2) A ninth capability `membership` (Member / Not joined / Banned / Needs you) is read from the community's own about data; it gates nothing and exists only where the connector can say it (`Connector.membership`, `PlatformInfo.canJoin`). 3) "Let the AI join r/x" is a mission the owner starts with one press; that press is the approval (no ticket: a ticket binds publishable content and there is none). The toolbox is the boundary, not the model: six tools (snapshot, navigate fenced to the community's pages, click, wait_for, ask_owner, report_result), one kind of click (a button, not submitting a form, whose whole name is Join, Join community or Join r/<community>), judged on the page as it is now and as the AI was shown it, on the platform's host and the community's own page, once per mission. A join mission is never also a post mission. 4) The AI's word never counts: after the engine ends the application reads membership itself (one retry), and only a read that shows the account as a member is "done"; the channel is then assessed again. 5) Only an engine that can be held to the application's tools (Claude Code) may do it; Codex is refused with wording for joining. 6) The application still never joins on its own: no schedule, no pipeline, no post mission joins.

## Reason

The owner expects the app to do what a person would do to be able to read and post in a community, but a click on an account that no approved text asked for is exactly what B1 forbids doing silently. An explicit press by the owner, a one-control allowlist enforced by the tool layer (the only boundary that holds against text on a web page), and a result the application verifies itself keep the rule's purpose and give the owner what they asked for.

## Consequences

The owner sees why a listing was refused and whether the account is a member. One button on the channel card starts the join; it can be stopped; it refuses banned accounts, private communities, members, signed-out accounts and engines that cannot be confined. Unproven until tried live: what Reddit's real Join control is called in the page snapshot (an unrecognised control makes the AI report needs_owner and nothing is pressed), the real answer behind the 403, and Claude Code driving a real join.
