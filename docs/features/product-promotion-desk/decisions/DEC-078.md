## Context

DEC-057 gave the accepted change of the weekly review to the AI's drafts as the owner's direction and said it changes no content mix. The sketch asked the owner whether an accepted change may steer the content mix by itself. On 2026-11-28 the owner answered: it changes the plan too ("Менять и план"). The content mix (DEC-033) is, per channel, three kinds of own post, each on or off with a rhythm in days; it prepares drafts only.

## Alternatives

1. Rewrite the channel's mix settings on accept: the owner's own values are lost and nothing restores them after two weeks
2. Let the AI write the days: a number supplied by the AI, against the rule that the application counts
3. Interpret the owner's own free-text change with another AI call: a second AI decision the owner did not see before pressing

## Decision

When the AI prepares the review it may add up to four plan steps to its one change, each in the mix's own words: a channel of the product named as the owner sees it, a kind of post, and one of three moves: more, less, pause. The AI never writes days. The application turns a move into a rhythm (more: half the days, or switched on at the kind's own days when it was off; less: twice the days, up to 90; pause: off), drops a step that names no channel of the product or would change nothing, and keeps each step with the owner's setting it was proposed against and what it becomes (weekly_reviews.plan_json). The review's change card lists the steps before the owner presses "Accept for the next week". A step is in force exactly while the accepted change is (the two weeks after the reviewed week) and only while the owner's own setting of that kind is still what it was: when the owner edits it, the owner's value applies at once and the review says the step is not applied. The channel's settings are never rewritten: the "Content mix" card keeps showing and saving the owner's own values and says beside a kind what the review made of it and until when. A change the owner wrote is free text and changes no plan. Declining changes nothing.

## Reason

The owner asked for the accepted change to reach the plan, not only the wording of drafts. Keeping the step as an overlay with an end date, instead of rewriting the channel's settings, makes the change reversible by itself, keeps the owner's own setting the lasting truth, and lets the owner's edit win without any reset. Letting the AI choose only among three moves keeps every number the application's.

## Consequences

Changes DEC-057 (the sentence "it does not change the content mix"). Drafts only, as in DEC-033: nothing is approved, scheduled or sent because of a step; the cap of three waiting drafts and the mix's other conditions stay. Unproven live: "Prepare the review" and "Accept for the next week" were not pressed on the owner's data, because they call the AI and save; proven by tests with a scripted engine.
