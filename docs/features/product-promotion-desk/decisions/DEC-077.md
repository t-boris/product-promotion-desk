## Context

Phase F of the publishing plan asked for Facebook's own schedule: a Page post made with published=false and scheduled_publish_time is published by Facebook itself, so a scheduled post goes out with the Mac off. Until now every scheduled item was sent by this application at its time, which needs the Mac awake (DEC-054). Meta's documentation (read 2026-11-27) gives the window (10 minutes to 30 days), the photo rules (temporary=true, unpublished_content_type=SCHEDULED), the list of kept posts (/{page-id}/scheduled_posts, is_published) and the views metric post_media_view (lifetime; needs read_insights and a Page with 100 or more likes). It does not say whether a post keeps its number when the schedule publishes it, and its pages disagree on whether every app may delete a post.

## Alternatives

1. A new send state 'scheduled on the platform' inside send_actions: about fifty places read that table and each would need to learn the state
2. Always hand over, with no switch back to this application: a post nearer than the platform's minimum, or refused by it, would not go out
3. A setting per channel to choose who keeps the schedule: one more thing to decide for no gain, the owner's time is kept either way
4. Leave scheduling to this application only (as before): the Mac must be awake at the time

## Decision

A connector may declare a schedule of its own (Connector.ownSchedule with the window, PublishRequest.publishAt, the answer "scheduled", readScheduled, withdrawScheduled, findScheduled). Only the Facebook Page connector declares it; every other channel works as before. An approved standalone post whose time is at least a quarter of an hour ahead (the platform's 10 minutes plus 5) and inside the window is handed over through the same gate as a send, with the account's limits counted at the post's own time and kept posts counted as sends. A kept post is one row of a new table platform_schedules, not a send attempt: the send (send_actions, confirmed, attempted at the post's time) is written when the platform has published it. The owner's own wish stays the rule: when the post is edited, moved, deleted, its channel paused or automation paused, the kept post is taken back from the platform first; before any send of the item by this application the kept post is taken back, and when that cannot be done the send is refused in words ("could publish it twice"). A lost answer leaves the row "not known" and the platform is asked what it holds; nothing is offered again for ten minutes. A kept post the platform no longer knows at its time is looked for on the page by its words before it is called unpublished; only then does it return to review. The scheduler looks at kept posts also while automation is paused. The owner sees it as words on the post's card ("Facebook keeps this post and publishes it at its time, even with this Mac off. Change or cancel it here and it is taken back first."); no new screen and no setting. Views of a Page post are read from insights with the reactions and comments; when Facebook does not give them the other two numbers are still read. The account's guide asks for a fifth permission, read_insights.

## Reason

A post that must go out at a time should not depend on one Mac being awake when the platform can hold it. Keeping the hand-over outside the send attempts leaves the sending invariants untouched (one attempt, one publish call, unknown blocks a resend) and makes "never twice" checkable in one place: a row exists exactly while the platform may still publish the item. The two things Meta leaves unsaid are handled instead of assumed, because a wrong guess there would publish twice or call a published post unpublished.

## Consequences

Unproven live: nothing was tried with a real Page (no Meta app yet). To see with the owner's token: a post kept in Meta Business Suite, taken back by an edit, published at its time and recorded; whether the number of the post changes; whether Facebook lets the app delete a kept post (if not, the card says to delete it on Facebook and Send stays refused); views on a Page with 100 or more likes. Replies are never handed over. A video to a Page is still not sent: Meta's current guide uploads through the app's number with a person's token, the one-request form names no host, and no page says how to get from the video's number to the post's number. Changes DEC-076 (which listed the schedule and views as not built).
