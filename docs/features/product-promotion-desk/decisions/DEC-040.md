## Context

Owner, 2026-11-15, after 0.21.0: the app cannot be told to look at every connected channel every hour or three hours, to look back at the first connection, or to say what is news. Until now a channel was checked every 30 minutes (default, per channel, Settings tab), the first read stored a baseline and created nothing, a cycle read the 25 newest discussions, a new post reached the Feed only when it named a topic of the channel word for word (topic-match.ts), and the product's goals never reached monitoring. The owner wants every post of a channel judged against the goal: by its title first (cheap), then, only when relevant, opened, read and labelled. Slice A (0.22.0) covers the channels the app reads through a connector (Reddit). The tab-per-task browser (slice B) and the read-only browser watcher for channels with no connector (slice C) follow as DEC-041 and DEC-042; plan in tasks/todo.md.

## Alternatives

1. Reuse opportunity states for notice and skip (rejected: every SQL state list and the Feed counts would treat them as work; a skip would flood the To decide count)
2. Triage with a browser walk for Reddit too (rejected for now: the connector is cheaper and steadier; the walk is for channels with no connector, slice C)
3. Judge only the posts that match a topic word (rejected by the owner: analyse everything, the AI decides relevance)
4. Keep 30 minutes as the default and let the owner change it (rejected: the owner asked for every 3 hours)
5. A fixed 90-day look-back (superseded: the owner said a month is enough)

## Decision

1. One function decides when a channel is next checked (nextCheckFor), used by the scheduler and by the monitor. A check starts up to min(20 minutes, 10 percent of the interval) earlier or later than that, derived deterministically from the channel and its last run (the mix.ts precedent), never sooner than one minute from now; injectable (Core.scheduleJitter, on in the application, off in tests) so channels never start together. A failed check keeps its own shorter retry and the scheduler's backoff. A shorter interval chosen in Settings moves the planned check earlier at once (never later). 2. The default interval is 180 minutes; migration 308 moves channels still on the old default of exactly 30 minutes to 180 (only where settings_json is valid JSON) and leaves every value the owner chose. The Settings tab offers Every hour, Every 3 hours, Once a day and Custom (5 minutes to a week). 3. A one-time look-back (settings.lookBackDays, 0 to 30, default 30; channels.looked_back_at, set once) reads the posts written since then through the new optional connector method listPosts (no comments, newest first, 1000 at most, 100 per page). Afterwards a pass reads the posts newer than an internal cursor (channels.listed_through) minus one hour; the cursor moves only after a listing that succeeded as a whole, to the moment that listing started, so a failed or interrupted listing is listed again next time and loses nothing. A listing in which any page fails fails as a whole. A listing that came back full says so ("looked back only to <date>"). The baseline rule stays for comments: no reply is ever prepared for an old comment. 4. Triage (src/core/pipeline/triage.ts): step 1, one AI call per batch of at most 40 titles (5 batches, 200 titles, per pass; the rest waits, newest first) decides what bears on the product's goals and the channel's purpose; step 2, for each relevant post (at most 10 read per pass, newest first, a post that cannot be read does not use up a read) the post and its top comments are read and one label is given: reply, notice or skip, with a one-line relation to the goal. A pass has a wall-clock budget of 6 minutes (the scheduler gives a run 10): no new call starts after it, nothing is charged for it. Goals reach the prompt for the first time. Page text is untrusted: it sits in fences, labels are enum-checked, numbering is checked, text is clamped and stripped of control and invisible characters, and nothing in it is obeyed. A note from the AI that holds a link or reads like an instruction is replaced by a fixed neutral sentence and the label stands (one such line never invalidates a batch). A post is judged once (findings, unique per channel and post). 5. reply opens the ordinary new_post opportunity (DEC-039) through the Ingestor with the post's comments stored as baseline, so identity, one voice (DEC-010) and auto-never-answers-a-new-post still hold, and the draft needs the owner's Approve; a post that is locked or archived (the Reddit connector now carries both flags), the account's own, or one where another login of the product already speaks becomes a notice that says why; a post older than a month is never a reply. notice is a finding the Feed lists as Noted; skip is a finding that is counted and listed only when asked (and pruned after 60 days). Nothing the triage does ever sends or approves anything. 6. The Feed shows the schedule line (Watching every ~3 h, last, next, or why not), a fourth list Noted, a First look marker on findings of the look-back, the count of posts judged not relevant with a list to audit them (counts follow the chosen channel), and how many posts wait for the AI. 7. The engine must be tool-free (a tool-using engine is never shown the posts of strangers: the posts wait, as they do when the engine is not ready). An answer that could not be used after one repair counts an attempt for its post, but only with evidence that the engine works: something was judged in the same pass, or any finding was judged by the AI in the last 24 hours. A failing batch is halved to find the bad title; when no half judges anything and nothing proves the engine, the failure is the engine's: nothing is counted and the pass ends. Later title batches are asked only if this pass itself judged something. After 3 counted attempts a post is settled as a notice that says the AI could not judge it, never dropped. A post whose thread could not be read is charged an attempt only when another thread was read in the same pass (otherwise the connector is down and nobody is charged); waiting posts are read fewest attempts first so a broken post cannot starve the others; after 3 it is settled as a notice that says it could not be read. Failures that are not answers (engine not ready, timeout, error, the owner taking the browser) are never counted.

## Reason

One schedule function removes two writers that could drift into lockstep. A cheap title pass keeps the AI cost bounded while every post is still looked at. Findings are a new table rather than new opportunity states because a notice and a skip are not work to approve, and opportunities carry approval, sending and history. Reading the look-back by listPosts keeps the heavy per-thread comment read for the posts that matter. The owner stays the only one who approves anything.

## Consequences

Unproven until the owner runs it on a real account: Reddit paging of new.json with the after parameter (the shape and the rate limits for up to 1000 posts), the cost and quality of the title pass on real titles, and how Claude Code answers the batch prompt. Limits: 5 title batches and 10 deep reads per pass, so a busy channel is judged over several passes (newest first); the app only runs while open (DEC-003), so a long closure makes channels overdue and they restart a few seconds apart. Every existing channel gets its first look once after the upgrade (up to a month back, within the same limits). Migration 308 changes the stored settings of channels on the old default of 30 minutes (a channel the owner set to exactly 30 moves too). An engine that breaks within 24 hours of a good pass can have up to 40 posts charged per pass; they are shown as notices, never dropped. A hostile or odd note from the AI on a reply label still opens an ordinary opportunity (with the neutral sentence) that needs the owner's Approve. A new finding label or stage is only partly compiler-enforced: the Feed counts and the queries filter by string. Channels with no connector (Instagram, Facebook, Threads) are still not read: that is the browser watcher of DEC-042. The Feed no longer shows the one-line summary after Check now (the watching line replaces it); the channel page keeps it. A check of a channel set to 5 minutes can start up to 30 seconds early because of the jitter.
