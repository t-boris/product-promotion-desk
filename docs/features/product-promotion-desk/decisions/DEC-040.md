## Context

Owner, 2026-11-15, after 0.21.0: the app cannot be told to look at every connected channel every hour or three hours, to look back at the first connection, or to say what is news. Until now a channel was checked every 30 minutes (default, per channel, Settings tab), the first read stored a baseline and created nothing, a cycle read the 25 newest discussions, a new post reached the Feed only when it named a topic of the channel word for word (topic-match.ts), and the product's goals never reached monitoring. The owner wants every post of a channel judged against the goal: by its title first (cheap), then, only when relevant, opened, read and labelled. Slice A (0.22.0) covers the channels the app reads through a connector (Reddit). The tab-per-task browser (slice B) and the read-only browser watcher for channels with no connector (slice C) follow as DEC-041 and DEC-042; plan in tasks/todo.md.

## Alternatives

1. Reuse opportunity states for notice and skip (rejected: every SQL state list and the Feed counts would treat them as work; a skip would flood the To decide count)
2. Triage with a browser walk for Reddit too (rejected for now: the connector is cheaper and steadier; the walk is for channels with no connector, slice C)
3. Judge only the posts that match a topic word (rejected by the owner: analyse everything, the AI decides relevance)
4. Keep 30 minutes as the default and let the owner change it (rejected: the owner asked for every 3 hours)
5. A fixed 90-day look-back (superseded: the owner said a month is enough)

## Decision

1. One function decides when a channel is next checked (nextCheckFor), used by the scheduler and by the monitor. A check starts up to min(20 minutes, 10 percent of the interval) earlier or later, derived deterministically from the channel and its last run (the mix.ts precedent), injectable and zero in tests, so channels never start together. 2. The default interval is 180 minutes; migration 308 moves channels still on the old default of exactly 30 minutes to 180 and leaves every value the owner chose. 3. A one-time look-back (settings.lookBackDays, default 30, 0 to 30; channels.looked_back_at) reads the posts written since then through the new optional connector method listPosts (no comments, newest first, capped); afterwards only posts newer than the last look. The baseline rule stays for comments: no reply is ever prepared for an old comment. 4. Triage (src/core/pipeline/triage.ts): step 1, one AI call per batch of at most 40 titles decides what relates to the product's goals and the channel's topics; step 2, for each relevant post (at most 10 per pass), the post and its top comments are read and one label is given: reply, notice or skip, with a one-line relation to the goal. Goals reach the prompt for the first time. Page text is untrusted: labels are enum-checked, text clamped, nothing in it is obeyed. A post is judged once (findings, unique per channel and post). If the engine is not ready the finding stays pending and is retried. 5. reply opens the ordinary new_post opportunity (DEC-039) through the Ingestor with the post's comments stored as baseline, so identity, one voice (DEC-010) and auto-never-answers-a-new-post still hold, and the draft needs the owner's Approve. notice is a finding the Feed lists as Noted; skip is a finding that is counted and listed only when asked. Nothing the triage does ever sends or approves anything. 6. The Feed shows the schedule (every ~3 h, last, next), a fourth list Noted, a First look marker on findings of the look-back (at most 30 per channel), and a count of posts judged not relevant that opens their list.

## Reason

One schedule function removes two writers that could drift into lockstep. A cheap title pass keeps the AI cost bounded while every post is still looked at. Findings are a new table rather than new opportunity states because a notice and a skip are not work to approve, and opportunities carry approval, sending and history. Reading the look-back by listPosts keeps the heavy per-thread comment read for the posts that matter. The owner stays the only one who approves anything.

## Consequences

Unproven until the owner runs it on a real account: Reddit paging of new.json with the after parameter and its rate limits for a month of posts, the cost and quality of the title pass on real titles, and how Claude Code answers the batch prompt. Limits: 10 deep reads and 200 posts per pass cap a busy channel, the rest waits for the next pass; a post whose engine call failed stays pending; the app only runs while open (DEC-003), so a long closure makes channels overdue and they restart a few seconds apart. Migration 308 changes stored settings of channels on the old default. A new finding label or stage is only partly compiler-enforced: the Feed counts and the queries filter by string.
