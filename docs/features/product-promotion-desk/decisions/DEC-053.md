## Context

The owner could set a time only on a post for a Reddit community the application reads. For every other channel (Instagram, Facebook, Threads, X, LinkedIn, Telegram, Pinterest, Reddit communities the application does not read) the time was refused, because a scheduled send never drove the AI browser (DEC-031 point 7, DEC-035, DEC-043); a reply of the Inbox could not be given a time at all; and an item more than one hour late returned to review. On 2026-11-27 the owner asked to "create posts and spread them over time" and set the goal to implement docs/design/publishing-api-and-schedule-plan.md, which answers its questions D1, D3 and D7 with the recommended defaults.

## Alternatives

1. Keep the rule and wait for official API connectors: no schedule on most channels for months, and never on channels that have no API (Reddit, Facebook profile and groups)
2. Let any automatic send drive the AI browser: a rule's approval is not the owner's reading of the text, so it stays refused
3. A fixed lateness of one hour: a Mac asleep overnight returns every evening post to review

## Decision

An item (post or reply) that the owner approved themselves and gave a time, with the approval given before that time, is sent when the time comes by the channel's own way, the AI browser included, with nobody pressing. This generalises DEC-049 from answers of the Feed to every post and reply and supersedes DEC-031 point 7 and the scheduling consequences of DEC-035 and DEC-043. An approval made by an automatic rule still never drives a browser, and an automatic send of an item with no time is still refused there. How late an item may still go out by itself is a setting (scheduleGraceHours, 1 to 48, default 6); later than that it returns to review as before. A channel has posting times (weekday and time of day, in the Mac's time), and "Next free time" puts an item on the first of them that no other unsent item of the channel holds; a posting time only chooses a time and never sends or approves anything.

## Reason

The owner's approval binds the exact content, account and destination, and the gate checks all of it again at the send and once more when the mission is about to submit; a time set by the owner on that approval is the same press made ahead of time, which DEC-049 already accepted for comments. Without it a scheduled post is possible on one kind of channel only, which is the opposite of what the owner asked for.

## Consequences

A send by the AI browser can now happen with nobody watching; it runs under the same guards as a pressed send, an unknown result blocks any retry until it is settled, and the run's steps are kept (DEC-051). Nothing is sent while the application is closed or the Mac sleeps (DEC-003 until phase B of the plan); the Send at control says so. The posting times live in the channel's settings (postingSlots), not in a table of their own. Unproven live at 0.51.0: a scheduled send carried by the AI browser on a real site.
