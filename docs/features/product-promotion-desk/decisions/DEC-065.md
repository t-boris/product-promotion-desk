## Context

Item 10 of docs/design/ideal-promotion-app.md (the owner's goal of 2026-11-27): the owner writes in one language and posts in channels of another; the draft helper keeps the draft's own language, so a translation could not be asked of it.

## Alternatives

1. A wish typed into Improve with AI: the helper is told to write in the draft's language, so the wish fights its rule
2. A translated version made automatically per channel language in a post group: not built; the owner translates the version they open

## Decision

The draft editor has a row "Translate into [language] · Translate" (the ten reading languages). The AI gets only the title and the text and the instruction to translate and nothing else: no fact added, markdown, names, handles and tags kept. The application refuses an answer that loses, changes or adds a web address or drops the title or the text. The translation goes into the editor with "Undo"; nothing is stored until the owner saves. It needs no approved passport, because it adds no statement about the product.

## Reason

Fewer steps outside the application; the same text often goes to communities of two languages.

## Consequences

The other content details of item 10 are not built: a topic tag for Threads, keywords for Pinterest, a carousel as a post form, a shot list for a video.
