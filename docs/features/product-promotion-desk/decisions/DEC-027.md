## Context

Threads has no sign-up and no password of its own: its sign-in page offers "Continue with Instagram". The preset only said so in a note, the app asked for an e-mail and a password for a Threads account, and the sign-in with the saved password filled a form the page does not have. The owner on 2026-11-14: Threads must connect to Instagram; the AI must log in through Instagram and not with a Threads password; an Instagram account must exist first; "it must understand the login on the Threads page, call the login from Instagram, and not ask me for Threads credentials at all".

## Alternatives

1. Share one browser profile between the Instagram and the Threads account so that Threads shows 'Continue as ...' by itself: breaks one profile per account and the aggregate-limit and session rules (DEC-015).
2. Let the AI type the Instagram password on any page of the mission: loses the by-host binding for a secret.
3. Ask the owner for a Threads user name and password: Threads has none.

## Decision

A platform preset can say `signsInWith` (Threads: instagram). An account of such a platform is created only with an existing account of that platform to sign in through (Account.signsInVia); without one the app says "add the Instagram account first". Such an account has no e-mail step, no password step and no password form; the owner is never asked for its credentials. Signing in is a mission of a new kind, `signin`: the AI opens the Threads sign-in page in the Threads account's own browser, takes the "Continue with Instagram" way, and signs in to Instagram with the login and password of the linked account, read from the vault for that one mission and handed over by name. The mission's hosts are Threads and Instagram; the password value is bound to the Instagram hosts and the toolbox refuses to type it on any other page, Threads included. The linked Instagram account cannot be removed while another account signs in through it. A CAPTCHA, a code or a check stays the owner's: the AI asks.

## Reason

The rule "a password is only put on a page of the account's own platform" stays true: the password of an Instagram account goes only to instagram.com, a host of the account it belongs to. Nothing new is stored: the owner keeps one password, in one place. The step order of the screen follows the real dependency: Instagram first, then Threads.

## Consequences

New column accounts.via_account_id; Account.signsInVia; accounts.create takes signsInVia, accounts.setSignInVia changes it; accounts.signIn is refused for such an account with the way to sign in; a secret value of a mission can carry hosts (TaskValue.hosts); agent.canStart/start take kind signin; the toolbox, the mission text and the account screen change; platform info carries signsInWith.
