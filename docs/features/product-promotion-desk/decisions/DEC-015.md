## Context



## Alternatives

1. Keep accounts inside products and allow sharing only with a confirmation (DEC-009 point 1): simpler, but not what the owner described
2. Make roles a separate list assigned to accounts and channels: more flexible, one more entity and screen; can be added later
3. Build the sign-up assistant before the mailboxes: it needs the address of the account first

## Decision

Accounts no longer belong to a product. They live in one library (a top-level "Accounts" screen) and a channel of any product picks an account of its platform. This replaces point 1 of DEC-009 (an account belongs to one product) and keeps its other points: the shared-account rules still hold (the SendGate counts limits per account across all its channels, one party at a time controls the account's browser), and the channels stay in the product page. The Accounts screen is only about accounts and shows no channels and no products; the link between an account and a channel is made, and its consequences are shown, where the channel is connected. A role stays a property of the account (DEC-010). The rule that two logins of one product on one platform may not play the same role now applies when a channel is connected, not when an account is created. Using an account that already serves another product needs no confirmation; the channel connect form says so and explains what it means. Every account has an e-mail address; the owner registers a base mailbox (an ordinary mailbox such as Gmail) and the application proposes a separate address for each account (an alias of that mailbox), different for every account. A "sign up" button opens the platform's registration page in the account's own browser; filling the form in for the owner comes later (DEC-014). Delivered in 0.3.0 (library, e-mail address, sign-up shortcut), 0.3.1 (the screen redone after the owner's remark: list and chosen account, steps in the order the owner works), 0.4.0 (mailbox connection, account inbox and one combined inbox), 0.5.0 (sign-up assistant).

## Reason

The owner wants to create accounts on their own, with an e-mail address for each, and to use them for channels of different products: accounts first, then channels linked to them. One account serving two products is the owner's choice; the risk (platforms and communities see one account promoting two products, which can look like spam) is shown on the screen instead of forbidden.

## Consequences

accounts.product_id stays in the database as information about the product the account was first created for and no longer restricts anything. The API loses reuseAccount and the account_other_product refusal; accounts.list always lists every account. A product's logins are now the accounts used by its channels. Migration 205 adds accounts.email and accounts.mailbox_id and a mailboxes table (address, kind); the IMAP connection of a mailbox comes in 0.4.0. Existing accounts and channels are untouched. REQ-008 and REQ-010 wording about a login per product is amended.
