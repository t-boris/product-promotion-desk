## Context

Threads files a post under one topic, typed into a field of its composer. Until now the application could only name a topic for the owner to copy ("Topic and keywords", DEC-072), and a post mission never touched anything of a form but its words and pictures. On 2026-11-28 the owner answered that the AI in the browser should type the topic into Threads' topic field. Meta's own page on Threads posts says: one topic per post, 1 to 50 characters, no periods and no ampersands. Threads has no connector in this application, so the AI browser is the only way a post goes out there.

## Alternatives

1. A separate tool that types the topic before browser_submit: text would sit in the form outside the held ticket, against the rule that nothing is typed without it
2. Putting "#topic" into the text of the post: Meta calls it the way kept for backward compatibility, it changes the owner's words, and the owner chose the field
3. Letting the AI type the topic: the AI would supply published text

## Decision

A draft has a topic (drafts.topic_tag), only on a platform whose composer has a topic field (Threads; PlatformForm.topic). It is kept clean (no leading "#", one line), refused when it is longer than 50 characters or holds a period or an ampersand, refused on a platform with no topic field and on a reply, kept when the words are rewritten, and part of the approval's hash when set, so changing it withdraws the approval. The post editor shows a "Topic (optional)" line on a Threads post, and on a new post when any chosen channel is Threads; "Propose with AI" names it from the post's text on the owner's press. A post the AI writes for a Threads channel comes with a topic (context.channel.topicRule, asked the same way as the link rule); one the field could not hold is left out. A post written for several channels gives the topic to its Threads versions only. On the site the AI only names the field (browser_submit's topic_ref): the application types exactly the approved topic, first, reads it back with the other fields and presses nothing when the form has no topic field, when the field named is not one the page calls a topic, or when the field does not hold the topic as approved. The preview shows the topic on the Threads sketch.

## Reason

The owner asked for it. Typing by the application, through the one road to a publication that already types the words, keeps the rule that the AI never gives or changes any part of what is published, and a topic that is part of the approval cannot differ from what the owner saw. Refusing instead of publishing without the topic keeps the other rule: never part of the approved content.

## Consequences

Changes DEC-072 for Threads (the topic is no longer only for copying) and adds one exception to what a post mission sets in a form, as the flair is one. Unproven live: the real Threads composer was not looked at; the name of its topic field ("Add a topic"), whether it keeps a typed topic as typed and whether it can be read back are assumed. Each difference stops the mission before the press with the reason in words, and taking the topic off the post lets it go out as before. When Threads' API is connected (phase G), the same topic is sent as topic_tag. Not built: a topic in the quick-post composer, and a topic proposed when the AI adapts one post for several channels (the owner presses "Propose with AI" on the Threads version).
