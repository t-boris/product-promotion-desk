## Context

DEC-003 kept automatic startup at login outside version 1 and ran nothing during sleep. Since DEC-053 the owner can give any post or reply a time, and a time is useless when the application is not running or the Mac fell asleep an hour before it. The owner's goal of 2026-11-27 answers question D2 of docs/design/publishing-api-and-schedule-plan.md with yes.

## Alternatives

1. Wake the Mac from sleep at the item's time (pmset schedule wake): needs administrator rights and a helper; not done
2. A separate background helper or launchd job that sends: the send ticket's secret lives only in the running application, so the sender must be the application itself
3. Start at login on by default: the owner did not ask for a program that starts by itself, so it is a switch they turn on

## Decision

Two switches in Settings. "Start at login" (off by default) registers the installed application as a login item; a start made by the login item opens no window and the application is in the menu bar. "Keep the Mac awake for scheduled items" (on by default) holds the system's idle-sleep blocker while something is being sent, and from before an approved, scheduled item's time until it has gone out or is too late to go out by itself: 12 hours before on mains power, 15 minutes before on battery; not while automation is paused. The rule is one pure function (awakeDecision) asked once a minute and on every change of settings or items.

## Reason

Without them a schedule works only while the owner sits at the Mac, which is when it is not needed. The 12-hour lead on mains power covers a post planned for the evening or the next morning; the short lead on battery keeps a schedule from draining a laptop in a bag.

## Consequences

This changes DEC-003: startup at login is now the owner's choice, and the Mac may be kept from idle sleep for the schedule. A Mac that already sleeps is not woken, and a closed lid still puts a laptop to sleep: the Send at control and the switch say so, and an item later than the lateness limit returns to review (DEC-053). Unproven live at 0.52.0: a real login after the switch is turned on, and the blocker over a real night.
