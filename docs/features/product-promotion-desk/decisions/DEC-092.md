## Context

Phase F of publishing-api-and-schedule-plan.md promises "video and Reels" on a Facebook Page through its API. Video posts were built in 0.89.0 (DEC-088). Facebook's guide "Reels Publishing API" documents a three-step upload and publish flow (`/{page-id}/video_reels` with `upload_phase=start`, raw upload to `rupload.facebook.com/video-upload/v27.0/{video-id}` with `Authorization: OAuth <token>`, `offset: 0`, `file_size`, and `/{page-id}/video_reels` with `upload_phase=finish`, `video_state=PUBLISHED`, and `description`) followed by status polling (`GET /{video-id}?fields=status`). On 2026-11-28 the owner chose option A on Q-002: an explicit "Video post" / "Reel" choice under the video, bound by approval.

## Alternatives

1. Let the application decide automatically by probing video dimensions and duration: adds a video reader and hides a publishing choice that Facebook's guide says the user makes.
2. Leave Reels out of the API and keep all Page videos as video posts: leaves the last part of phase F unbuilt.

## Decision

Drafts store `video_as` (`'reel'` or null, migration 410), bound into `contentHash` when set and carried over across text rewrites while the post still holds one video. Under the video of a post for a Facebook Page API channel (when the post has one video and no picture), the editor shows a "Video post" (default) / "Reel" choice with Facebook's limits beside it (9:16, 3-90 s, at least 540 x 960). The preview notes "The video goes out as a Reel on Facebook." before approval. Sending a Reel runs Facebook's three-step Reels Publishing API flow and polls status every 5 s for up to 5 minutes until published or Facebook names a processing error; lost or slow finishes reconcile through `GET /{page-id}/video_reels`. Reels with pictures beside them, Reels on replies, Reels on non-Reel channels, and Reels for Facebook's own schedule are refused before sending; a scheduled Reel goes out from this Mac at its time.

## Reason

Matches the owner's choice on Q-002 and Facebook's documented Reels Publishing API, while keeping approval binding intact and leaving existing video posts unchanged.

## Consequences

Migration 410 adds `drafts.video_as`. Unproven on a real Facebook Page: the upload and processing on rupload.facebook.com, whether a published Reel appears in the Page's feed for comments and numbers, and Facebook's 30 Reels/day limit (any refusal is shown in Facebook's words).
