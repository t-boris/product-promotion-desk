## Context

Phase F of the publishing plan waited for proof T2 (is a post made by an app in Development mode seen by others) and T3 (how one owner gets a token without a server). Meta's documentation, read on 2026-11-27, answers both for a Page: a post made in Development mode is seen only by the people of the app until the app is switched to Live; a Page access token taken from me/accounts with a long-lived token of the person does not end. The owner has no Meta app yet, so nothing was tried with a real Page. The owner asked that the application itself gives the steps, with addresses, every time an account is connected, Meta included.

## Alternatives

1. Sign in with Facebook Login inside the application (an authorisation window, the app's id and secret kept in the vault, the exchange done by the application): fewer steps for the owner, but a second mechanism beside the pasted key, and Meta's pages do not say that a loopback address is accepted
2. Wait for the owner's Meta app and proof T2 before writing any code: the plan's first rule, but the answer does not change the connector
3. Keep a Page in the account's browser only: no sending with the browser closed, numbers and comments read by the AI about once a day

## Decision

Built like the Telegram bot (DEC-055): a Facebook account whose "How the app works with it" is "Through a Page (Facebook's own API)" holds one key in the vault, the Page access token, and its channel uses the connector `facebook-page`. Saving the token asks Facebook whose it is: the account takes the Page's name; a token of a person, or one that ended, is said in words. The channel is the Page's own address (by name or by number; a share link, a group or a post is refused), and the channel's check says when the token belongs to another Page. The connector posts words, a link (as the link of the post) and photos (one, or several as one post); reads the Page's last 25 posts with up to 100 comments each at every reading, so comments become items to answer; replies under a comment or a post as the Page; finds a post whose answer was lost in the Page's feed; counts reactions and comments under a post through the existing Results reading, and followers as the channel's number. It keeps nothing of its own: Facebook holds the posts. The account's guide has eight steps with the addresses on developers.facebook.com: register as a developer, create an app with the use case "Manage everything on your Page", add four permissions, get a token in the Graph API Explorer, extend it in the Access Token Debugger, take the Page's token from me/accounts, publish the app (Go live), paste the token. Phase F was built before proof T2, as phase E was before T1: the answer changes the last step the owner does in Meta's dashboard, not the connector.

## Reason

The owner's goal (the publishing plan) and the owner's order that connecting an account is guided inside the application. One key per account is the mechanism that already exists; a Page token that does not end needs no refresh job and no app secret in the application.

## Consequences

Nothing was tried with a real Page: the names of Meta's screens in the eight steps (the use case, "Customize", "Extend Access Token", "Go live") come from Meta's documentation and from memory of the tools, and may differ. Not carried in this slice: a video (refused before the send, in words), Facebook's own schedule (scheduled_publish_time, so the Mac has to be on at the time, as for every other channel), views of a post (insights). The names of commenters need the permission pages_read_user_content; without it a comment is from "Somebody on Facebook". A reading asks Facebook up to 26 times. A Page's posts by visitors are read as posts to answer.
