## Context

Item 8 of docs/design/ideal-promotion-app.md (the owner's goal of 2026-11-27): the application heard only the channels it is connected to.

## Alternatives

1. A paid listening service or search API: needs the owner's key and money; the installed engines already search
2. Watch competitors' own pages in the browser on a schedule: heavier, and the any-site watch already does this for a page the owner adds as a channel

## Decision

On the Review screen a card "Heard elsewhere" keeps up to five phrases and five competitors per product; the product's name is always watched. "Look now" is one AI request with web search for recent pages of three kinds: a mention of the product or a phrase, talk about a competitor, a place where the subject is talked about and the product is absent (a channel idea). The application keeps each address once, drops a find that names no watched term or is the product's own site, marks what the last look added as new, and never offers a dismissed address again. After the owner's first look the scheduler looks again when the last look is a week old. A find is a page to read: nothing is posted, joined or added as a channel from the card, and its text is strangers' text, shown plain and never followed.

## Reason

The research: small products are talked about where their owner is not present; hearing it is the cheapest source of channel ideas and replies.

## Consequences

What is found depends on the engine's search and is not complete. A place found is not turned into a channel by a press yet. No Inbox item is raised by a look.
