## Context

The first version needs a concrete platform for validating its complete promotion workflow.

## Alternatives

1. Facebook as the mandatory first platform.
2. A specific forum as the mandatory first platform.

## Decision

Use Reddit, with one user-selected subreddit, as the mandatory first platform for Natal Mystic. The required workflow includes reading discussions, publishing approved standalone posts, and sending replies under the application's authorization rules.

## Reason

The user selected Reddit from the proposed platform alternatives.

## Consequences

Other platforms remain within the connector scope, but their end-to-end operation is not required for first-version acceptance.
