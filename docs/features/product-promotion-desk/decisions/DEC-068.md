## Context

Item 11 of docs/design/ideal-promotion-app.md asks for a warm-up plan for a new account. The application does not know an account's age on the site.

## Alternatives

1. Guess the account's age from the site: not read anywhere today
2. Hold posts with a link while the plan runs: a block the owner did not ask for (DEC-050)

## Decision

The Health card of an account has "Warm-up plan for a new account" with "Start the plan": 14 days and 10 replies before the first post with the site's address (the application's own defaults). It shows "Day 3 of 14 · 4 of 10 replies sent", says when a post with the site's address already went out, and becomes Done when both the days and the replies are there. The owner stops or removes it. It is a reminder: it blocks, warns at and changes no send.

## Reason

The owner knows which account is new; the application can honestly count only what it sent itself.

## Consequences

Replies the owner writes by hand outside the application are not counted. The numbers 14 and 10 are constants in shared/account-health.ts.
