## Context

The plan's phases G and H list "hide" among what the Threads and Instagram connectors do; 0.82.0 and 0.84.0 left it out. Meta documents both: Threads hides a reply to the account's post with `POST {reply-id}/manage_reply` (`hide=true|false`), only a reply to the post itself and with every reply under it, and says of each reply whether it is hidden (`hide_status`); Instagram hides a comment under the account's post with `POST {comment-id}` (`hide`), and says so in the comment's field `hidden`.

## Alternatives

1. Leave hiding to the site: the plan's item stays unbuilt
2. Hide from the Feed's thread as well: the Feed shows posts of other people on channels worked in a browser, where nothing can be hidden through an API
3. Let a rule or the AI hide what looks like spam: the owner decided that nothing is decided by the AI
4. Also delete a comment: not in the plan, and it cannot be undone

## Decision

On the card of an item that answers somebody else's comment, on a channel that works through the Threads or Instagram API, a button "Hide on Threads" / "Hide on Instagram" stands under the comment, with what the press does said beside it before it is pressed (other readers stop seeing it, on Threads with every reply under it; nothing is deleted). One press sends the one request under the account's lock; after it the comment is marked "Hidden" and the button is "Show again". A refusal is said in the platform's words and nothing is changed. On Threads a reply to a reply is not offered the button, because Threads hides it only with the reply it is under. What the platform says of a hidden comment when the channel is read replaces what is kept, so hiding or showing on the site itself is seen here. Only the owner's press hides: no rule and no AI proposes or does it. A connector says that it hides comments (`hideComment`, `hidesTopLevelOnly`); the card asks the application whether and how (`commentHiding`).

## Reason

The plan asks for it, both requests are documented, and it is the owner's own act on their own page. One mechanism on the port serves both platforms and any later one. Hiding does not close the item: whether to answer, defer or reject it stays the owner's separate decision.

## Consequences

Migration 409 adds `comments.hidden`. A Facebook Page's comments are not offered the button (the plan does not list it; Meta documents `is_hidden` for a later step). Unproven with real apps: both requests, and that Threads lists a hidden reply in a post's conversation with its `hide_status`. Seen on a screen in an own instance with example data: the button, its words, and the reason said when no token is saved.
