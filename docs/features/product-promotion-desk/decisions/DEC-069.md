## Context

Item 10 of docs/design/ideal-promotion-app.md: carousel as a post form and a shot list for a process video; the application does not produce video.

## Alternatives

1. Draw the slides as pictures: a picture maker of its own; not built
2. A separate post type 'carousel' bound by the approval: changes the publishing path on sites not proven live

## Decision

The draft editor has "Plan this post": "As a carousel" and "As a short video". The AI cuts the post's own text into 3 to 10 slides (headline and text) or shots (what to show, what to say), in the post's language, adding no fact and no web address. The plan is shown under the editor with "Copy the plan"; nothing is stored and the draft is not changed. The owner makes the slides or the video.

## Reason

The document's own limit: the application helps to plan the form, it does not make pictures of slides or video.

## Consequences

A carousel still goes out as a post with several pictures the owner attaches. The Threads topic tag and Pinterest keywords stay with the open tags question for the owner.
