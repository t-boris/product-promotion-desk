## Decision

Add read-only IMAP mailbox connectivity (imapflow + mailparser, owner-approved) and a unified Mail Inbox under Accounts. Migration 214 extends mailboxes with IMAP settings/status and creates a header-only mail_messages index storing ONLY headers and redacted previews (stripping URLs, codes and long tokens). IMAP app passwords are stored exclusively in the encrypted vault under mailbox:<id> and never appear in SQLite, logs, API outputs, or AI prompts. Full plain-text bodies and verification codes/confirmation links are fetched on demand over IMAP in memory for the owner only; webmail links open in the app's own tabbed browser via accounts.openAddress. Includes Gmail/iCloud/Fastmail/Yahoo presets and an Outlook.com/Hotmail personal OAuth2 note.

## Reason

Owner approved adding imapflow and mailparser (plus @types/mailparser) and completing REQ-013 (Mailboxes and unified Mail Inbox) so platform verification mail and account notices can be read safely inside the app without leaking credentials or storing sensitive codes/links on disk.
