## Context

Item 7 of docs/design/ideal-promotion-app.md (the owner's goal of 2026-11-27): promotion by a person is a set of relationships; nothing remembered who was met, what was said, what was promised, or whose words about the product may be quoted.

## Alternatives

1. Collect people automatically from the Feed's comments: a store of strangers' handles made without the owner's hand; not built
2. Give approved testimonials to the AI's posts: a quote stated by the AI needs a rule of its own; not built
3. An Inbox item per thing owed: it would nag; Owed is a filter on the screen

## Decision

A screen "People" in the sidebar, per product: a list (search; filters All, Owed, Testimonials; the people owed something first) beside one person's form: who, platform, where met, what was said, what I owe ("Done, nothing owed" clears it), their words about the product, the address where they said it, and the permission to quote (not asked, asked, given). Only words with permission given are marked usable. Every entry is typed by the owner. Nothing of it is given to the AI, used in a prompt or posted; the application quotes nobody by itself. A permission without words is not kept. Built from a published sketch on the goal's standing instruction.

## Reason

The research: a memory of people is what no posting tool has, and a testimonial without permission and source cannot be used honestly.

## Consequences

No "Remember this person" press on a Feed comment yet. Testimonials are not offered in the post editor. The owner may still move the screen (sidebar item or a tab of the Feed).
