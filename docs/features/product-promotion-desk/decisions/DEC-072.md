## Context

Item 10 of docs/design/ideal-promotion-app.md: a topic tag for Threads and keywords for Pinterest. Whether the application itself puts tags into a post is an open question with the owner (the Reddit editor and tags note).

## Alternatives

1. A topic field on the draft that the AI browser fills in the Threads composer: changes what an approval binds and the publishing path, and waits for the owner's answer on tags

## Decision

"Topic and keywords" beside "Plan this post" in the draft editor: the AI names one topic (one to three words) and 3 to 10 search phrases from the post's own text, in its language, without "#", repeats or web addresses. Shown with Copy; nothing is stored, the draft is not changed and nothing is put into a post by the application. The owner adds them where the platform takes them.

## Reason

It gives the words without deciding the open question of who types tags into the site.

## Consequences

Pinterest is not a publishing channel until phase J; the keywords are for the owner's own pins.
