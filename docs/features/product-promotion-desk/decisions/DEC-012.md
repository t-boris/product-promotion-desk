## Context



## Alternatives

1. A list with one goal marked as the main one: more screen and logic with no use yet, since goals do not steer anything in version 1
2. A goal per channel in addition to product goals: needs a link between goals and channels and changes the Channels tab; can be added later without breaking the list

## Decision

A product keeps a list of promotion goals (up to 20). Each goal has its own target action (required), success metrics (one per line) and notes. Goals are added, edited and removed one at a time on the product's Goal tab, and the Results tab lists all of them. The single goal stored by versions up to 0.1.5 becomes the first item of the list by a migration.

## Reason

The owner asked why only one goal is possible. REQ-006 and DEC-002 already say "promotion goals" in the plural, and a product such as Natal Mystic has distinct goals (create a first chart, join the newsletter, come back on day two) with different metrics; the single goal object was an implementation simplification, not a decision. The owner chose a plain list over a list with a main goal and over goals per channel.

## Consequences

Data: products.goal_json is replaced by goals_json (migration 101, existing goal kept). API: products.setGoal is replaced by products.saveGoal and products.removeGoal; the Results view returns a list of goals per product. In version 1 goals still do not steer drafting and do not measure conversions (DEC-002). Version 0.1.6.
