## Context

Until 0.18.0 the app prepared drafts of replies on its own (the monitor) and a post only when the owner pressed Suggest (a product post that cites approved facts). The owner (2026-11-15) asked that, besides replies, the AI should from time to time propose the persona's own posts ("I did this", news, a showcase) on each channel, spread over Reddit, Instagram, Facebook and Threads. ChannelPlanStep.frequency is free text that nothing reads. Approved on 2026-11-15 with the plan: the AI may create own-post drafts on a schedule, DRAFTS ONLY (never approves, never sends).

## Alternatives

1. The mix as a global setting of the product: a persona on Reddit and one on Instagram need different rhythms, so it lives with the channel
2. The AI invents what the persona 'did': an own update made of nothing is a fabricated claim by a login that is meant to be honest (DEC-010), so the source of an own update is the owner's note, the way approved claims are the source of a product post
3. A job that also approves or sends what it prepared: REQ-003.5, DEC-008 and the owner's B1 rule say the owner approves every post and only the owner's press sends
4. A calendar the owner fills slot by slot: more work for the owner than the one thing they asked for (say what the project is about, the AI does the rest)

## Decision

(1) The mix is a setting of the channel (ChannelSettings.mix: a list of {kind, enabled, everyDays}, off by default) with kinds product_post (the existing Suggest path), own_update (new) and showcase (a product post told to show one concrete thing the product does); replies stay what the monitor does. (2) The owner's notes ("What's new": one line whenever they did something) are a small journal per product. An own update is written ONLY from one unused note, in the voice of the channel's login (role, and the profile's about and purpose), by a second system prompt that may state only what the note says; a draft that brings a number, a link or a name that neither the note nor an approved fact contains, or that the model itself says adds facts, is blocked by the same safety path; the disclosure rule of DEC-010 applies unchanged when the text names the product. (3) A scheduled job (mix.run, next to the digest and the metrics sweep) looks for what is due, with a cadence that has a small steady jitter, and prepares at most one draft per channel per scan. It prepares a draft and nothing else: state draft, no approval, no send; it does nothing while automation is paused, while the gate is closed, when the channel is observe-only or cannot publish, when the engine is not ready, and when 3 posts of that channel already wait undecided. (4) The AI proposes the mix: the plan's steps carry a structured kind and everyDays beside the free-text frequency, and 'Let the AI propose a mix' fills the card from them for the owner to edit and save; nothing is enabled by the AI.

## Reason

The owner asked for the AI to carry the rhythm of own posts without being told each time, and the same owner's rules forbid the AI to speak for the persona without a basis or to publish without a press. The note is the basis, the cap of 3 waiting drafts keeps the queue from becoming a chore, and every guard of the single post path (approval, hash, gate, disclosure, limits) is untouched because a mix draft is an ordinary draft of an ordinary standalone post.

## Consequences

New: migration 305 (product_updates, mix_state), the mix and updates API, the Content mix card on the channel, the notes list, the mix job in PeriodicTasks, a second prompt and a note-bound check in draft-prompt and publications. An own update the owner never wrote a note for cannot exist; an own update draft is reviewed, edited, approved and sent like any post (also in a group). The job spends the owner's AI quota in the background: the cap of 3 waiting drafts and the retry gap bound it, and the card says when the next draft is due. Unproven until used: how good the drafts are on the owner's products and personas.

## Update 2026-11-15 (during the build)

- A note the AI could not turn into a post three times (`product_updates.failed_tries`) is set aside, so one bad note never holds the others back; the card says so ("The AI could not make a post from a note. Rewrite it, or remove it, and tell what is new.").
- The cap of 3 counts the channel's own standalone posts in `draft` or `awaiting_approval`, never replies and never approved posts.
- When an own update is due and no usable note exists the job stays quiet and the attention list asks "Tell what is new for <channel>" (derived on demand, gone as soon as a note exists). It asks for words; it posts and approves nothing.
- Own updates never state a price: the approved facts are not offered to them, so a price in the note alone is not enough to let one through (a price is for the posts that cite their fact).
- Review fixes (2026-11-15, an independent reviewer): a failure of the AI itself (down, slow, stopped) no longer counts against a note and ends the scan; a note is tried once per scan however many channels want it; the kind that has waited longest goes first; a note whose post was rejected or deleted before any send is released; the wait after a failed try grows (3 h, 6 h, 12 h, 24 h); a last draft in the future (a clock set back) counts as now; the scan stops between channels when automation is paused; an unapproved profile is not read; number words, other digits and ways of writing a thousand are compared; the note's bare domains count and an address on the note's host must be the one the note gives (or below it); a model that does not say whether it added a fact is refused. Not checked by any rule: names of people, places and products (the model's flag and the owner's reading). Known and left: a draft committed and then a crash in the same instant before `mix_state` is written would allow one more draft after the rhythm (bounded by the cap of 3); a look-alike letter or a fullwidth dot in an address is not read as an address by the older safety check (the owner approves every draft).
