## Context

DEC-086 left replies on X unread, on the ground that every read is paid. The plan the owner approved already counts on them: "replies read through own mentions", at X's price for an account's own data ($0.001 a post or a mention when the app belongs to the account that signs in; price list read 2026-11-28). X's rule for an answer made through its API: it is taken only when the answered post names the answering account; a reply to the account's post does, unless its writer took the name out.

## Alternatives

1. Leave replies to be read and answered on X (DEC-086): nothing of a conversation reaches the Inbox
2. Read every tracked post's conversation through search: $0.005 a post and only seven days back
3. Collect every post that names the account as an Inbox item (the inbox mechanism): posts of strangers that only name the account are not conversations under the owner's posts; left out
4. Keep what was read in the database instead of in memory: avoids one full reading after each start, more code; can follow when the cost shows

## Decision

The X channel is read. The application keeps, while it runs, the account's own posts and answers and the posts that name it, of the last 30 days, and asks X only for what is newer (the account's own list of posts and its mentions, the two addresses priced as own data). A post of the account with the replies that name it is a discussion; a reply becomes an item to answer; the account's own answers are its own. The owner's answer is sent under the reply; when X refuses it, the refusal is said with X's rule and "Answer this one on X". The numbers of posts are read from the account's own list of posts as well ($0.001 a post a day, about $0.03 a post in 30 days, instead of $0.005). The price of an answer ($0.01) is said where an answer is approved (the review card and the answer from the Feed), asked of the application by the screen. A connector that itself says the inbox is unavailable keeps its own reason on the channel's card, so a channel whose replies are read as comments no longer says they are not collected (this also corrects the cards of Facebook, Instagram and Threads channels worked through an API).

## Reason

The plan asks for it and its price was part of the plan the owner approved. Asking only for what is newer and through the own-data addresses keeps the cost at a tenth of a cent for each new post or reply. Every reply read this way names the account, so X's written rule allows the answer; whether X's servers agree for a reply under the owner's own post is proof T4, which needs a real app, and a refusal is said in words instead of being hidden.

## Consequences

After each start of the application the 30 days are read once in full (X charges a post once a day). A reply whose writer took the account's name out is not seen. X's price list does not say whether the name of a reply's writer, which comes with the reply, is charged as a person read ($0.01): the steps say so. Not built: collecting posts of other people that only name the account; a video; a quote. Unproven with a real X app, the answer rule (T4) included.
