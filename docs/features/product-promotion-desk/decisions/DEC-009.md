## Context

The owner pointed out during implementation (2026-11-08) that the product determines which channels exist for it. Platforms such as Facebook are a shared catalog, but enabling a channel is done per product, with its own login for each product on each platform, and results are reported per product. The owner also expects the AI to help create a new channel, or at least to give instructions for it. Records checked: SRC-001 section 1 (one account may serve several channels and several products), section 3 (a channel may be suggested by the AI; the universal connector may create a declarative site profile; several products may share one account), section 6 (the eight global screens), REQ-002, REQ-004 (aggregate limits for shared accounts), DEC-001..DEC-008, discussion.md. The built application had global accounts, a global Channels screen, a Results screen that lists every product at once, a hard-coded platform list and no way to suggest channels or to save a universal-site profile.

## Alternatives

1. Strictly one login per product with no reuse (rejected: the owner chose to keep reuse possible)
2. A product switcher in the top bar that scopes the existing eight screens (rejected: the owner chose Channels and Results inside the product page)
3. AI writes instructions only and the user fills in the site profile by hand (rejected: the owner chose suggestions plus a profile draft)

## Decision

1. Account scope (SUPERSEDED by DEC-015: accounts are one library shared by all products; the shared-account rules named here stay in force): an account belongs to one product on one platform and has its own login and browser session. A new account is created per product by default. The user may deliberately reuse an existing account of another product; in that case the shared-account rules of SRC-001 section 1 and REQ-004 stay in force (aggregate limits, one party controlling the browser session at a time). 2. Navigation: Channels (accounts, capabilities, rules, schedules) and Results move into the product page as tabs next to Sources, Passport, Analysis and Goal, and leave the main menu. The main menu becomes Attention, Products, Opportunities, Publications, Log, Settings (DEC-015 adds Accounts as a menu item of its own). Opportunities and Publications stay top-level, show the product and channel on every item and get a product filter (the owner did not move them; this is an assumption to confirm in the walkthrough). SRC-001 section 6 is amended accordingly. 3. Platforms are a shared catalog (prepared connectors plus the universal connector), not product data; the platform list comes from the connector registry. 4. AI help for a new channel: from the approved passport the AI suggests channels as unverified ideas (addresses are checked during assessment); for an unknown site the AI only reads the site and prepares a draft declarative profile of the universal connector, which is schema-validated, versioned and saved only after the user reviews it, never executed as code, with capabilities left as needs verification until real use; when no profile can be produced the app shows step-by-step manual instructions.

## Reason

It matches how the owner thinks about the work (product first, channels per product, own login per product) while keeping the SRC-001 rules for the case where one account is deliberately reused, and it delivers the AI-assisted channel creation that SRC-001 section 3 already describes. All three choices were answered explicitly by the owner.

## Consequences

Accounts gain a product reference; accounts created before the change are assigned to the product of their channels and keep working. Channel and result API calls become product-scoped. The renderer routing and menu change. New API for channel suggestions and for drafting and saving a universal-site profile. REQ-008 carries the testable criteria. The independent review fixes already under way do not change.
