## Context

Item 7 of docs/design/ideal-promotion-app.md (the owner's goal of 2026-11-27): the first two days under a post decide whether a conversation lives; the application read the numbers but said nothing when a post ran far above the usual, and a comment to the account looked the same in the Inbox after an hour and after three days.

## Alternatives

1. A separate summary item counting waiting comments: they are already items of the Inbox, a second item would repeat them
2. Let the AI judge that a post took off: a number against the channel's own usual needs no judgement

## Decision

Both are worked out by the application from what it read; the AI has no part and nothing is stored. A post of the last four days whose points or comments are at least twice its channel's own usual (the average of at least three earlier posts of the last ninety days) and above a floor of 10 points or 5 comments is an Inbox item "A post took off in <channel>" with the numbers and the usual; it opens the channel's Results tab and goes away four days after the post. An Inbox item about a comment addressed to the account (a reply to it or a mention) says how long the comment has waited once that is twelve hours, and after two days that an answer this late is rarely read.

## Reason

The research: reply speed in the first 48 hours is the one conversation habit every platform rewards.

## Consequences

Works only where the application reads post numbers (Reddit today) and where the account's inbox is read. Comments under a post on a browser-worked channel have no time of writing, so no waiting note there. Testimonials with permission and the people met are not built yet.
