## Context

DEC-042 watched channels of five platforms (Instagram, Facebook, Threads, X, LinkedIn) through the browser; any other unread channel was parked as "your own posts only" and never observed. Reddit answers 403 to the post-list address for every client that is not its own application, even without a sign-in, so the Reddit connector could not read any community's posts; rules and numbers still worked. The trend search existed only inside the quick-post composer, for three platforms.

## Alternatives

1. Keep the list of five platforms and add Reddit to it (rejected: the owner wants any forum or site)
2. Read Reddit through its public RSS feeds (rejected: also refused or limited, and one more access path to keep working)
3. Register a Reddit application and use its OAuth (rejected: the app works as the owner's own signed-in browser, never with credentials of its own)
4. Let the AI reply to what it finds (rejected: the owner writes everything; the AI is the eyes)

## Decision

(1) A channel the app cannot read itself (no connector, no site profile, or a community whose post list the platform refuses) is no longer a channel "for own posts only": the AI looks at it in the browser of the channel's account, like a person, read-only, on the usual schedule, and what it finds goes to the Feed as notices. This holds for every platform and site, not for a fixed list of five. The list BROWSER_WATCH_PLATFORMS is removed; Channel.watchedInBrowser is exactly "not reads". (2) Where the AI may go comes from the channel's own address: a platform's own domains when the app knows them, the site of the address otherwise; for a Reddit community also its newest posts, best of the week and its own search; for an unknown site only the channel's page and the links the AI sees there on the same site (web-places.ts). (3) Reddit: the connector answers "does not read" when its assessment found the post list refused (HTTP 403/401, an HTML page instead of data); that community is then watched in the browser and activates instead of failing with "Comments cannot be read on this channel". A later assessment that finds the list readable reads it through Reddit again. (4) Look around: on any channel's Status tab three buttons, "What people talk about", "What is happening now", "Analyse the feed", send the AI to look (the same read-only research mission as the composer's trend search, now with a kind of look); it shows the topics or patterns it saw and one angle to write from. Nothing is saved, posted, replied to or changed. (5) Posting is unchanged: the owner writes and approves every post and presses Send; the AI only reads and helps word and picture.

## Reason

The owner, 2026-11-21: "we must support any forum, any site we find", the Reddit community r/astrology could not be monitored ("Comments cannot be read on this channel"), "we can open the browser like in Instagram or Threads", and wants search variants for trends, what is happening and feed analysis. Then: "the AI does nothing for me, I do it myself. I read only through the AI's eyes. I write everything myself."

## Consequences

Every channel the app cannot read is now looked at on its schedule (default every 3 h with jitter), which uses the AI engine and the account's browser; a channel can be paused. A channel stored as "own posts only" before this starts to be watched at its next check. The words "Your own posts only" no longer appear for any channel the core makes. A forum with no search the app knows is looked at through its page only. Not proven live: the real r/astrology look, a real forum, and the three kinds of look on real pages; the AI's judgement on real posts.
