## Context

DEC-056 kept the rule "the connector reads; the AI never supplies a number", so posts made in the browser (Instagram, Facebook, Threads, any site) had no numbers: such a post has no platform id and often no address. On 2026-11-27 the owner said: likes and comments under posts are not "may be read", they must be read.

## Alternatives

1. Read the counts by application code from each site's markup, as on Reddit: a selector set per site that breaks with every redesign; the owner asked for no per-site fixes
2. Ask the owner to type the numbers: not reading
3. Wait for Meta's API (phases F-H): only professional Instagram accounts and Pages, and not built

## Decision

Changes DEC-056 for channels whose connector has no reader and whose account works in a browser. A "numbers look" is a watch mission with the same six reading tools (no click, no typing), whose record tool is numbers_record instead of watch_record: the AI opens the account's own page (and the post's own address when known), finds the owner's sent posts by their text and date, and records likes, comments and views for each. The application keeps only: a post of the brief by its number; whole counts from 0 to 100 million (a count not shown is left empty, never zero); and when the page of the post itself was looked at, the application's own reading of that page's text must contain the first words of what was sent, or the numbers are refused. The post's address is saved only in that verified case. At most one look per channel in 20 hours by itself, for the posts of the last 30 days that are due (10 newest); "Read now" on the channel's Results tab starts one at once. Paused channels and paused automation are not looked at. A trial on such a channel can count likes and comments.

## Reason

The owner's order. Reusing the watch mission keeps one barrier (read-only tools, the platform's own domains) and one place where the account's browser is taken and given back.

## Consequences

A number read from a list page (where the post's own page was not opened) rests on the AI's reading alone. Each look is an AI session in the account's browser, so numbers on these channels are about a day old. Not yet seen working on a real site.
