## Context

Item 1 of docs/design/ideal-promotion-app.md: goals were text only, so no goal showed how far it is. Visits, sign-ups and sales need the site's analytics, which waits for the owner's answer (DEC-002 still holds).

## Alternatives

1. Let the owner type the current value by hand for visits or sales: a number the application cannot check; not built
2. Wait for the analytics connection: leaves every goal as text

## Decision

A saved goal can get "Set a number and a date" in Setup: what to count (posts sent, replies sent, points and likes under what was sent, comments received under it, people added to People), a whole number, and a day. The count starts when the target is saved; a changed number or day keeps it, another measure starts it over. The goal shows "12 of 40 comments received · 18 days left" with Running, Reached or Not reached. A removed goal takes its target with it. No AI is asked. Also: "Remember" on a comment in the Feed opens the People form filled with the author, the channel and the words (nothing is kept until Add), and "Add as a channel" on a find of "Heard elsewhere" opens the add-a-channel flow on that address with the platform told from the site.

## Reason

A goal with a number the application can honestly count is better than none; it never claims visits or sales.

## Consequences

The Overview does not show the progress line yet. When analytics is decided, visits and sales become further measures.
