## Context

REQ-006 includes inspection of connected business events and allows analytics setup to be deferred, but no first-version ingestion route is specified. F-002 identifies this undefined delivery scope.

## Alternatives

1. Implement manual CSV import of Natal Mystic business events.
2. Integrate one selected analytics service.
3. Retrieve business events through a Natal Mystic API.

## Decision

Version 1 will display available platform metrics and retain promotion goals. Business-event integration is deferred to a later version.

## Reason

The team selected deferral to establish an explicit first-version scope without introducing an additional event source, ingestion workflow, or integration dependency.

## Consequences

Update REQ-006 to retain promotion goals and available platform metrics while explicitly excluding business-event ingestion and analytics connections from version 1. Results must distinguish unavailable metrics from zero values and must not imply measured business conversions. Resolve F-002 through this explicit scope boundary. A later version will require a separate ingestion and attribution contract.
