## Decision

Complete browser watching B4 (own posts first + ongoing comment watching on browser-watched channels) and add multi-tab support to the owner's account browser window. For B4, BrowserWatchFlow syncs recent confirmed standalone sends on the channel into findings (is_own = 1), places recent own posts FIRST in watch briefs and excludes them from already_judged, and refreshes comments under own posts on subsequent watch checks (resetting seen_at to unread only when new comments arrive, staying strictly read-only). For tabbed browsing, UserBrowserWindow hosts multiple WebContentsView tabs in the same account session with a slim tab strip (+ new tab, click to switch, × close tab, site popups opening in a new tab) while preserving DEC-038 multi-window semantics and keeping ownerPage/pageReport aimed at the active tab.

## Reason

Owner asked to finish all remaining Section 6 work including B4 (own posts and their comments watched first on channels the app cannot read) and a tabbed account browser window.
