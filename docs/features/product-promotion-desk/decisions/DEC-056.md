## Context

The owner asked on 2026-11-22 for likes and views to be collected and shown; only channel-level Reddit readings existed, nothing per sent post. Item 1 of docs/design/ideal-promotion-app.md (the owner's goal of 2026-11-27) starts with it.

## Alternatives

1. Let the AI read the numbers from the page: a wrong reading cannot be told from a right one (lesson 298)
2. One reading at send time only: a post's numbers grow for days

## Decision

For every confirmed post of the owner's own, the connector reads what the site shows under it (likes or points, comments, views) on a schedule that follows the post's age: first after 15 minutes, hourly in the first day, four times a day in the first week, daily for 30 days; a removed post is not read. The latest reading stands on the send, every reading is kept for the line over time. Shown on a new Results tab of the channel (totals, a row per post with its line, Read now), on the Posts row and on the post's card. A number the site does not show is null and its column is not drawn; nothing is a zero by default. The AI never reads the numbers. Reddit is the first reader (the account's own list of posts: points and comments, no views).

## Reason

Without a number per post the owner cannot tell which post worked, and the weekly review (item 2) has nothing to compare.

## Consequences

Channels worked in the browser (Instagram, Facebook, Threads...) have no reader yet and the tab says so; a reader per platform, the post link on those platforms, the tagged site link and the site's own analytics (which reverses DEC-002 and needs the owner's answer on which analytics the sites use) are later slices. Telegram gives a bot no views.
