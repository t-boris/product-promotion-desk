## Context

A comment written in the Feed's reply window must be quick: the AI writes it, the owner edits it, then chooses Send now (the AI browser types it), Schedule (the application does the same at a chosen time) or Edit in full (the full card with picture, link and formatting). Two old rules forbade this: "a send nobody presses never drives a browser" (sendWays) and "an owner-approved reply is sent only on a press" (gate not_automatic). Both were written for sends made by automatic rules and for scheduled posts, not for the owner's own press made ahead of time. Separately, a community whose rules forbid replying (publish_reply restricted) held the owner's own send with no way through, which left a stray card in To decide. Owner (Boris Tsekinovsky), 2026-11-21: yes to "Send anyway" for community-rule refusals on the owner's press only; automatic sends keep the rule.

## Alternatives

1. Keep 'scheduled never drives a browser' and send scheduled comments by a connector (not possible: the channel is one the application does not read)
2. Make Schedule only a reminder for the owner to press Send later (rejected: the owner asked for the app to do it later)
3. A global switch that turns the community rules off (rejected: the owner wants it per text, on their press)
4. Send anyway for automatic sends too (rejected by the owner: automatic sends keep the rule)

## Decision

1) An answer from a post of the Feed that has a planned time AND the owner's own approval (approved_by user) is sent by the scheduler (Sending.sendDuePublications) through the AI browser without a press, with the same guards as a scheduled post: global automation pause, approved before the planned time, more than an hour late goes back to review. The two old rules yield ONLY for finding_id + scheduled_at + user approval (scheduledByOwner in send-ways.ts); rule-made approvals, scheduled posts and every other reply are unchanged. 2) "Send anyway" / "Schedule anyway": an approval made by the owner's press may carry acknowledged = [community_rules]; the gate then ignores a restricted publish capability for that approval only (an unavailable or needs_user capability, a paused channel, limits and the owner's hold on the browser still hold). The acknowledgement is stored on the approval (migration 318), dies with the approval when the text is edited, and an automatic approval never carries it. 3) Opportunities with finding_id are not cards to decide: they are left out of the To decide list, the Inbox, the Feed tab counts and the Overview waiting counts, and live only in the reply window of their post. 4) The AI never adds a link to a comment (reply); a link the owner wrote stays.

## Reason

The owner's own press, made ahead of time on exactly the text they approved, is still the owner's decision; the old rules protected against unattended sends the owner never approved. Keeping the rule for everything else and changing it for exactly what the owner asked keeps the safety of automatic modes intact. Letting community rules through is the owner's knowing choice for one text, not a standing switch.

## Consequences

The application now posts, with no press, at the planned time, an owner-scheduled comment from the Feed through the AI browser (unattended send for this one case). A held scheduled send is retried each scheduler tick until it is an hour late, then goes back to review. Scheduled posts keep the old rule. Rule-made approvals never gain an acknowledgement.
