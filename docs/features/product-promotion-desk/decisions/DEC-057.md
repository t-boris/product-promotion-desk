## Context

Item 2 of docs/design/ideal-promotion-app.md (the owner's goal of 2026-11-27). Since DEC-056 the application has numbers per sent post, but nothing turned a week of them into a decision about the next week.

## Alternatives

1. Let the accepted change alter the content mix by itself: the owner has not said yes to that; the mix stays the owner's setting
2. Compare with other accounts or market benchmarks: no honest source for them
3. Several proposed changes at once: nothing can then be attributed

## Decision

A Review screen per product, one week (Monday to Sunday, the computer's time) at a time. The application counts posts and replies sent, what the sites showed under the posts and what was taken down, for the product and per channel, against the average week of the four before; a number no site showed is left out, never a zero. On Monday 9:00 (or on the owner's press) the AI reads those numbers and the titles of the posts and writes what worked, what did not and ONE change; a week with fewer than two sends is not reviewed. The owner accepts the change, declines it or writes their own. The accepted change is given to the AI as the owner's direction when it proposes posts for that product, for two weeks after the reviewed week; it never overrides the writer's rules. It changes no schedule and no content mix and nothing is posted because of it. Earlier weeks list their change and the numbers of the week after.

## Reason

The research behind the document: a small promoter improves by a short weekly ritual that compares against their own baseline and changes one thing at a time.

## Consequences

The layout was published as a sketch and built on the goal's standing instruction before the owner answered it; the place in the sidebar, the day and hour, and whether the change may steer the content mix remain the owner's to change. Channels the application cannot read numbers on show counts only.
