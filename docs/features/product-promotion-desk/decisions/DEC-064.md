## Context

Item 11 of docs/design/ideal-promotion-app.md (the owner's goal of 2026-11-27): accounts are lost when most of what they post is their own link, and nobody notices that reach fell until weeks later.

## Alternatives

1. A warning or a block above a share of ten percent: the owner decided that only what a site enforces stops a press
2. A warm-up plan for a new account: the application does not know an account's age on its platform; not built

## Decision

The account's page shows a card "Health", counted by the application on demand and stored nowhere. First: of the posts and of the replies the account sent in the last thirty days, how many carry an address of the product's own site (in the link field or in the text), as a count and a percentage. Second, per channel of the account: the average points or likes of the latest three posts against the average of the posts before them (at least three, within 120 days), said as lower, about as before or higher; lower is at most half. The card attaches no rule and no warning to the numbers and stops nothing (DEC-050 stands).

## Reason

The research: the link share is the simplest early sign of an account read as promotional; a fall in reach is the first sign of a quiet restriction.

## Consequences

Reach is known only where post numbers are read (Reddit today). No Inbox item is raised when reach falls. The warm-up plan is not built.
