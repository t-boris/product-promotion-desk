# DEC-042: Channels without a connector are watched in the account's browser, read-only

Status: accepted (owner, 2026-11-15: "I need to check this as soon as possible").
Released: 0.23.0.

## Decision

A channel of Instagram, Facebook, Threads, X or LinkedIn has no connector (`reads=false`, DEC-034). It is now WATCHED anyway: `Channel.watchedInBrowser`
(derived, never stored: `reads` is false and the platform is in `BROWSER_WATCH_PLATFORMS`). On the DEC-040 schedule (default every 3 h with jitter, first look
back `lookBackDays`) the AI opens the channel in the account's browser like a person, judges each post against the product's goals and topics and records
FINDINGS only (Feed: Noted and "Judged not relevant"). Nothing is replied to, measured, baselined or sent from what it reads; publishing is unchanged.

## How

- Mission kind `watch` (`AgentRunService`), started only by the watch flow (`src/core/pipeline/browser-watch.ts`, called from `MonitorFlow`); Claude Code only
  (an engine that cannot be confined to the application's tools does not run it; the check says why and is deferred).
- Read-only is enforced in the tool layer, not in the prompt: the watch toolbox has exactly `browser_snapshot`, `browser_scroll`, `watch_open`, `watch_back`,
  `watch_record`, `report_result` (`WATCH_TOOL_NAMES`). `watch_open` goes to a link of the last snapshot on the platform's hosts, or to a listed place (the
  channel address, the first 3 queries as the platform's own search); it never clicks. No type, select, key, evaluate, upload, submit or ask-owner tool.
- `watch_record` takes up to 20 items per call; `reply` is stored as a notice ("Worth a reply: ...") because the app cannot reply there; an unsafe note is
  replaced by the fixed neutral text; known URLs are ignored.
- A busy browser, a service that is not ready, an unconfinable engine or the owner stopping the AI defers the check quietly (retry in 5 minutes, nothing
  suspended, no failure counted). A login or CAPTCHA wall suspends the channel and marks the account. Failed or timed-out looks use the usual backoff.

## Unproven (issue #8)

Real Instagram, Facebook, Threads, X, LinkedIn pages: the search addresses, what counts as a post address, whether the six tools are enough to read a real post,
whether the platforms accept this pace, and the AI's judgment on real posts. Only fake pages and a scripted AI were used.
