## Context

Item 4 of docs/design/ideal-promotion-app.md (the owner's goal of 2026-11-27). The passport holds facts with their sources; nothing said where the product stands against what people use instead, and the AI wrote in the product's vocabulary, not the audience's.

## Alternatives

1. Positioning as a ninth section of the passport: its answers would become statements the AI may state as facts
2. Keep quotes with their authors: the bank is words, not people; no personal data is needed
3. An FAQ store with approved answers per question: not built; the answer is a post draft for now

## Decision

Two steps are added to Setup, neither a stage of the launch path. Positioning: five answers (what people use instead, what only this has, what that gives, who cares most, what kind of thing it is). The AI proposes them from the approved passport, marking a guess with "Assumption:"; the owner edits and approves; a new proposal waits beside what is in force. Approved, they are given to the AI's proposed posts as the angle; they are the owner's judgement and never facts: the rule "state only approved statements" stands above them. Audience's words: after a check of a channel (at most every six hours) or on a press, the AI takes questions, objections and phrases in the people's own wording from the posts and comments the Feed read in full (not the account's own posts), each kept with its posts; the same thing said again joins the kept entry; no names, handles or links are kept. The most repeated are given to the AI's proposed posts as vocabulary, as data only. A question or objection said in three posts or more is marked, and "Prepare a post that answers it" makes an AI draft for a chosen channel, approved like any other. An entry the owner removes is hidden and not offered again.

## Reason

The research: positioning comes before posting (Dunford's method), and drafts land when they use the audience's own words and answer what is asked repeatedly.

## Consequences

Only the AI's proposed standalone posts read positioning and the words; reply drafts, the composer's helpers and the content mix's own updates do not yet. No FAQ entity exists. A channel the application does not read in full contributes nothing to the bank.
