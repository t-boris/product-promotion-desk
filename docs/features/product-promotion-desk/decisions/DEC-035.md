## Context

Owner's screenshot 2026-11-15: "Failed to post to instagram, do not change profile... look like it doesn't control the browser... it should be exactly like human - this is for QA so clicking scrolling are required". The AI never started: the app refused at Approve because the Settings switch "Let the AI publish in the account's browser" (publishWith, default connector) was off, although no connector can post to Instagram, Facebook or Threads (DEC-034: channel.reads = false). The window in the screenshot was the owner's own Open browser window. Separately, the AI browser could not scroll, saw the page as one long list, and moved without a visible pointer, and nothing forbade it to press a profile control or give a file to the profile photo's input.

## Alternatives

1. Keep the switch and only explain it better at Approve (rejected by the owner: the switch had no purpose for these channels)
2. A separate consent switch per not-read channel (rejected by the owner: Claude Code, the exact approval and the owner's own Send press are the consent)
3. Let scheduled or automatic sends use the AI browser for these channels (rejected: unattended sends never drive a browser, DEC-031)
4. Make the AI's report plus a screenshot the evidence that the post is up (open question to the owner; not done: it would change DEC-031's rule that the app's own finding, never the AI's word, confirms a send)

## Decision

1) For a channel the app cannot read (reads = false) the AI browser is the only way to publish, so it needs no switch in the settings. It is used when ALL hold: the send is the owner's own press on an approval the owner gave themselves (never a scheduled send, never an automatic approval), the engine is one that can be held to the application's browser tools alone (supportsAgent and confinesAgent: Claude Code), and the build has the post-mission service. The same refusals apply to an older (non-markdown) draft, because they depend on the channel's way and not on the form of the post. Otherwise Approve refuses with the reason and the way out ("choose Claude Code in Settings (AI engine), or use Copy post"); nothing is guessed and nothing is sent. The switch stays for channels the connector can read, where it only decides about what a connector cannot carry (several pictures, pictures with words on Reddit). 2) A post mission never touches the account's own profile (it neither presses, chooses, types into nor gives a file to such a control; a post is never typed into a field named like a bio, a username or a phone number, nor into a form that holds one; the words are matched in English, Russian, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, German, French and Ukrainian, and code-style names are read apart): no click on, and no choice of, a control that is about the profile (photo, name, bio, username, phone, password, settings, cover; English and Russian), and no file given to a file input that the page says is for the profile, judged by the input's own name OR the text around it, on both what the AI was shown and what the page shows at the press. The check comes before the post-setting check, and the click list of what a post may press is as restricted as before. 3) The AI works the browser like a person: a browser_scroll tool (mouse wheel, a screen or half a screen, up or down, held to the platform's hosts like every step), a visibly moving cursor that glides to the target and pauses before a click, real wheel turns and real mouse moves, and a snapshot that lists what is on the screen first with the rest marked "(off screen)" and says where the screen is on the page.

## Reason

The owner connects these platforms only to post on them, and the only thing standing between a not-read channel and its post was a switch that nothing else could satisfy: asking for it was a refusal with no way forward. The safety did not live in the switch but in the confined engine, the ticket-bound submit, the owner's own press, the approved content and the closed list of what may be clicked; those all stay. A profile control is a destructive, irreversible account change that no post asks for, so it is refused by name and by context, and the file input of a profile photo is the one file door a post mission must not use. QA needs the AI to be seen doing what a person does (scrolling, pointing, pausing), and a screen-first snapshot keeps a long page from hiding what the AI needs.

## Consequences

A post for such a channel cannot be scheduled (the AI browser carries only a send the owner presses). A post for Instagram, Facebook or Threads can be approved and sent with the Settings untouched, by the owner's press, through Claude Code; with Codex or no engine Approve says what to choose. The result of such a send stays 'unknown' until the owner confirms the post, because the app cannot read these platforms back. Profile-looking controls are refused in a post mission even when a real post step is named alike; the words are matched whole and checked against the real site's names, which is unproven (live acceptance, issue #8). The join mission gets browser_scroll too (seven tools). A press follows a fixed order so that nothing slow comes between the last look and the press: the pointer glides to the element and rests (a hover, before the lease commit for the final submit), and after the point of no return the press is `still` (only where the pointer rests: nothing scrolls, glides or waits; an element that left the point is not pressed and the commit is taken back); the watch that tells whether a real press arrived is armed right before every press; before every move, wheel notch and press the agent's hold on the browser is checked, so the owner taking over stops it at once. The page's own file input and open dialog are listed first in the snapshot, and a file input's `near` text is read from the two nearest containers that say something. Unproven until the owner tries: real Instagram, Facebook and Threads publishing by the AI browser, the profile guard against the real sites' DOM, and the injected cursor overlay on real sites. Proven on local pages in real Chrome and Electron: real wheel turns and mouse moves, a visible cursor, screen-first snapshot, profile controls and the profile photo input refused, scrolling to an off-screen Create.
