## Context

Question 1 of docs/design/ideal-promotion-app.md. The owner answered on 2026-11-27: it depends on the product; sometimes Google Analytics or Plausible, sometimes nothing at all; a measurable goal about site visits cannot be set where there is no analytics. This changes DEC-002 (no analytics connection) for products whose owner chose a service.

## Alternatives

1. Only a yes/no 'the site has analytics' with a number typed by hand: a number the application cannot check
2. Read Google Analytics now: an OAuth flow and a cloud project per owner; not built

## Decision

Setup, Goal step, card "Site visits": a choice per product, "No analytics on the site" (the start, nothing asked), "Plausible" or "Google Analytics". With Plausible the owner gives the site's domain and a Stats API key (kept in the vault, never shown, logged or given to the AI; an own Plausible address is optional); the application reads unique visitors of the last 30 days once a day or on "Read now", and the visitors that came by the tagged address of a post, by platform and channel. Google Analytics is noted as a fact; the application does not read it. The goal measure "Visitors of the site" is offered only where the application can read visits, is asked from the service at most once an hour, and keeps the last count with the reason when the service does not answer. Business events (sign-ups, sales) are still not taken in.

## Reason

The owner's answer: per product, and nothing is a valid state. Plausible needs one key and one request; Google Analytics needs a Google Cloud project and a sign-in flow, which is a piece of its own.

## Consequences

A post has no tag of its own, so visits are known per channel, not per post. Reading Google Analytics is not built. Not proven against the real Plausible: the owner has not connected one.
