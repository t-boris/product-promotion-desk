## Statement

The system shall organise channels, accounts and results around the product: each product enables its own channels with its own login per platform, shows only its own channels and results, and offers AI help for connecting a new channel.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] An account is created for a product and a platform, belongs to that product and has its own persistent browser session; a product's Channels tab lists only that product's accounts.
- [ ] A channel can be connected with an account of another product only after an explicit confirmation; the shared-account notice, aggregate limit and single-controller rule then apply.
- [ ] Channels and Results are tabs on the product page and are absent from the main menu; each shows only the selected product's channels, accounts and results.
- [ ] Opportunities and Publications show the product and the channel on every item and can be filtered by product.
- [ ] The platform list offered when adding an account comes from the registered connectors (prepared platforms plus the universal connector), not from a fixed list in the interface.
- [ ] For a product with an approved passport the application asks the AI for channel suggestions and lists them as unverified ideas; choosing one pre-fills the connect form, and nothing is connected, assessed or published until the user continues.
- [ ] For an unknown site the AI prepares a draft universal-connector profile from the site's content; the draft is schema-validated, shown to the user, saved as a new version only after the user accepts it, and never executed as code; its capabilities stay needs verification until proven by real use.
- [ ] When the AI cannot prepare a profile or no engine is available, the application shows step-by-step manual instructions for connecting the channel.
- [ ] Accounts and channels created before this change keep working after the migration and are attached to their product.
