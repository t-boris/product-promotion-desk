## Statement

The system shall organise channels and results around the product: each product enables its own channels, using any account of the platform from the shared account library (DEC-015), shows only its own channels and results, and offers AI help for connecting a new channel.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] An account has its own persistent browser session and belongs to no product (DEC-015); a product's Channels tab lists the accounts its channels use and those made for it, and any account of the library can be used for a new channel.
- [x] A channel can be connected with an account that already serves another product without a confirmation; the screen warns, and the shared-account notice, aggregate limit and single-controller rule apply.
- [x] Channels and Results are tabs on the product page and are absent from the main menu; each shows only the selected product's channels and results.
- [x] Opportunities and Publications show the product and the channel on every item and can be filtered by product.
- [x] The platform list offered when adding an account comes from the registered connectors (prepared platforms plus the universal connector), not from a fixed list in the interface.
- [x] For a product with an approved passport the application asks the AI for channel suggestions and lists them as unverified ideas; choosing one pre-fills the connect form, and nothing is connected, assessed or published until the user continues.
- [x] For an unknown site the AI prepares a draft universal-connector profile from the site's content; the draft is schema-validated, shown to the user, saved as a new version only after the user accepts it, and never executed as code; its capabilities stay needs verification until proven by real use.
- [x] When the AI cannot prepare a profile or no engine is available, the application shows step-by-step manual instructions for connecting the channel.
- [x] Accounts and channels created before this change keep working after the migration and are attached to their product.
