## Statement

The system shall check every channel it can read about every 3 hours, each check starting a few minutes earlier or later than that so that channels never start together; shall look back at most a month at a new channel and read only what is new afterwards; and shall judge every post it finds by its title against the product's goals, open and read only the relevant ones, and label each reply, notice or skip with a one-line relation to the goal, without ever sending or approving anything.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A new channel checks every 3 hours by default; the owner can choose every hour, once a day or a custom number of minutes of at least 5
- [ ] The next check of a channel is the interval plus or minus at most min(20 minutes, 10 percent of the interval), the same for the scheduler and the monitor, and never sooner than a minute from now
- [ ] The first check of a channel reads the posts written in the last lookBackDays days (0 to 30, default 30) once; later checks read only newer posts
- [ ] A post is judged once: by its title in a batch of at most 40, then, when relevant, read (at most 10 per check) and labelled reply, notice or skip with a relation of one line
- [ ] A reply opens the ordinary new-post opportunity under all existing rules (identity, one voice, no automatic answer to a new post); a notice is listed as Noted; a skip is counted and listed on request
- [ ] The posts, titles and the AI's words are untrusted: they never cause a write on the platform, an approval or a send
- [ ] When the AI engine is not ready or is not tool-free the posts wait and the Feed says how many; an answer that cannot be used is retried and, after 3 counted attempts, shown as a notice that says the AI could not judge it
- [ ] The Feed says how often the app watches, when it last looked and when it looks next, or why it does not
