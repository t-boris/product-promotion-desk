## Statement

Connect channels through prepared or universal browser connectors and expose capabilities established for each current connection. First-version acceptance must demonstrate the complete Natal Mystic workflow in one user-selected Reddit subreddit, including discussion reading, approved standalone publication, and authorized replies.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] For Natal Mystic, a user can connect one selected subreddit and persist its account, session, settings, and assessed capabilities across application restarts.
- [ ] The selected subreddit supports reading posts and comments, detecting new discussion activity, publishing user-approved standalone posts, and sending replies under the configured authorization rules.
- [ ] Required outgoing actions are verified through actual authorized use; onboarding does not publish test messages to a real community.
- [ ] Sent posts and replies have a confirmed result under the existing sending-verification rules; an unresolved outcome cannot count as a successful acceptance action.
- [ ] Capability assessment remains specific to the current account and channel. Support for other platforms is reported only to the extent actually verified.
