## Statement

Turn relevant discussions and promotion opportunities into context-aware drafts, review tasks, or explicitly permitted responses.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Repeated discovery updates an existing comment rather than creating another response opportunity, and the account's own comments do not trigger automatic reply chains.
- [ ] Response preparation uses the original post, available conversation context, prior account replies, current product knowledge, and channel rules.
- [ ] Channels support observation, draft preparation, approval, and restricted automatic response modes.
- [ ] Restricted automatic responses are limited to explicitly enabled simple categories supported by approved knowledge; ambiguous or sensitive requests require user participation.
- [ ] Independent publications support text, links, and images where verified by the connector, and always require user approval.
