## Statement

The system shall keep the owner's accounts in one library independent of products, give each account an e-mail address proposed from the owner's base mailbox, and let a channel of any product use any account of its platform.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] An account can be created without choosing a product and works for channels of any product.
- [x] The Accounts screen is only about accounts: a list of every account (name, platform, sign-in state) and, for the chosen one, its role, e-mail address, password and sign-in in that order with the next step to take; it shows no channels and no products.
- [x] A channel of any product can be connected through any account of the same platform without a confirmation; when the account already serves another product, the channel connect form says so and explains the consequences (one shared allowance, one party controlling the browser, one account promoting two products).
- [x] Two logins of one product on one platform may not play the same role: connecting a channel through an account whose role name another login of that product already plays on that platform is refused; the same role name may exist for different products.
- [x] The owner registers a base mailbox, and the application proposes for each account an address of its own, different from every other account's; the owner may change it.
- [ ] A sign-up button opens the platform's registration page in the account's own browser, with the address and a generated password at hand; nothing is filled in or submitted by the application.
- [x] Accounts created before this change keep working and appear in the library.
- [x] An account that a channel uses cannot be removed.
- [x] A new account is added from one short form on the Accounts screen (platform, name, optional e-mail address, optional role); "Use this role" on a product page opens that form with the role filled in, and no button of a form submits it except its submit button.
- [x] The platform of a new account is always chosen by the owner (no default), and an account whose name mentions another platform than its own says so on its card.
