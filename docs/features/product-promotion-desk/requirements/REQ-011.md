## Statement

The system shall offer named platforms besides Reddit and any other website, serve them through the universal connector, and let the AI propose what to do on each connected channel as advice that the application never executes.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] The platform list offers Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and other named platforms in addition to Reddit and any other website; an account can be created for each.
- [x] A channel on a named platform is connected by its address or by a short handle, and an address outside the platform's own domains is refused.
- [x] A named platform is not reported as working until a reviewed site profile exists and its abilities were actually seen; before that every ability is 'needs check'.
- [x] On request the AI proposes a plan for a connected channel from the approved passport, the promotion goals and what the platform accepts: steps with formats, what the owner prepares, how often, and what to check first.
- [x] A plan step that does not fit what a post on the platform can hold, or that fakes or forces engagement, automates posting, bypasses limits or promises results, is dropped.
- [x] A plan is advice only: nothing is assessed, drafted or published because of it, and it can be replaced by asking again.
- [x] Photos and video are brought by the owner; the application does not create media and does not upload files in this version.
