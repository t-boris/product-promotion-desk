## Statement

The system shall let a product use several logins on one platform, each with its own role and voice, shall never let two logins of the product speak in the same discussion, and shall disclose the author's connection to the product in drafts that mention it.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] A login can have a role (name and voice) that the owner sets, changes and removes; a login without a role uses the channel's reply style.
- [x] The AI can propose roles and username ideas from the approved passport; the application does not register accounts and never sees or stores passwords.
- [ ] Drafts for a channel are written in the voice of the login connected to that channel, and the draft's basis shows which role was used.
- [ ] A draft that mentions the product states the author's connection to it in one short phrase and remains editable before approval.
- [x] At most one login of a product speaks in a discussion: while one login has a draft awaiting approval, an approved item or a sent message there, another login of the same product gets no reply opportunity and cannot send there, and the reason is shown.
- [x] A comment written by any known login of the product never creates a reply opportunity for any login of that product.
- [x] Connecting a second login of a product to the same community is allowed, and the screen explains that only one login speaks per discussion.
- [x] The one-login-per-discussion rule is enforced by the send gate at send time and cannot be switched off.
