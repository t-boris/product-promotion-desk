## Statement

When the owner creates an account on a platform, the system shall have the AI propose the details to register it with (e-mail address, login name, display name, short description) from the approved product passport, the account's role and the platform, and shall offer an icon (avatar) for the account; nothing is registered or submitted by the proposal.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The proposal is advice: the owner reviews and edits every value before anything is used, and nothing is typed into a site by the proposal itself.
- [ ] The proposed e-mail address is made from a registered base mailbox and differs from every address already in use.
- [ ] The proposed login and display names follow the account's role and the platform's naming rules, and are marked as unchecked: the platform may refuse them.
- [ ] An icon can be offered for an account; the owner sees it before it is saved and can replace or remove it.
- [ ] The proposal uses only the passport and the role, never a password or the content of any mail.
- [ ] When the AI engine is not ready or the passport is not approved, the owner is told why and can fill in everything by hand.
