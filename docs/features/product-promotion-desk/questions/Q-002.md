## Question

How does the owner say that a video going to a Facebook Page through its API is a Reel and not a video post?

## Why it matters

The plan's phase F says "video and Reels"; the video post is built (0.89.0) and a Reel is the one plan item left. Facebook's guide "Reels Publishing API" (read 2026-11-28): a Reel is a separate request with its own limits (9:16, 3 to 90 seconds, at least 540 x 960, 30 Reels a day), a video that does not fit is refused during processing, and the guide says the user of an app chooses. The plan does not say where the choice is made, and it is a control the owner would see on every post with a video to a Page.

## Options

- **A**: A choice under the video of a post to a Facebook Page: "Video post" (as now, chosen by default) or "Reel", with Facebook's limits for a Reel said beside it. The approval binds the choice. Nothing changes for a post where the choice is not touched.
- **B**: The application decides: it reads the video's shape and length and sends it as a Reel when it fits Facebook's limits, and as a video post otherwise; the preview says which before the approval.
- **C**: No Reels through the API: a video to a Page stays a video post; a Reel is published on Facebook itself.

## Recommended

A
